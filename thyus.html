Thyus

A player character remembered through Tales of the Old Six.

Known History

Thyus at a young age knew his mother hated him. He was never taught anything nor was he taken care of. He remembers another child with him at the time he doesn't remember his name, he was also a tiefling, it doesn't matter he was sold off when he was five. He lost his eyesight sometime after that his mother poured chemicals over his face as punishment he doesn't remember much after that all he knows is that he was thrown out a few days after. About seven days later, blind starving, and stuck in an alleyway he was found by an old elven fighter and taken in by him that day. He taught him everything he knows from how to speak elvish to living without his eyesight. He feels more elven than he does human nowadays. Thyus learned how to fight five years after he was found by his new adoptive dad, Thyus started out training with swords, glaives, axes, and any melee weapon he even just used his fist. Nothing felt right and he could never get the stances right; it never worked. The first time he picked up a firearm he was fifteen, his dad finally gave up when he gave Thyus the firearm. Grumbling about how “this isn’t tradition” and “this isn't how true fighters fight”. When he grabbed the gun it just felt right like it was meant to be; cows give milk, his mother is a massive bitch, Thyus was meant to hold a gun. He spent the next five years of his life practicing to be a gunslinger and honing his senses to be ready for when he finally left. (fun fact: Thyus actually made himself deaf for a day the first time he shot a bullet he couldn't walk around for the entire day without tripping on something)

Thyus was twenty when he left, before he left his mentor gave him one last thing. A pale white mask that covers his whole face and his ear with an ear protector in it. He took it with a smile on his face and thank you on his lips. Thyus tried to be an adventure but he didn’t like it very much. After that, he went on to spend the next five years being a mercenary for hire. Taking the usual merc jobs like taking down a bandit camp, killing a rogue knight, killing a rogue adventure, among other things. Thyus was twenty-five when it happened, he was on the road back home cause a job he took led back through his hometown. When he got home it had already happened, the distinct smell of blood wafted through the air like a thick fog. Thyus couldn’t see but he could smell that multiple people died earlier that day. He didn’t care; he could already smell that his father was dead. After that day he stopped doing mercenary work and just lived at his home. It was two months later when they came with a job offer.

I went back to where I was sitting before we had the meeting with a shellshock look on my face ‘I didn’t even know these people existed before today now he’s telling me one of these “sinners” killed my mentor’ i think as I take a sip from the drink I had before leaving my table ‘and he thinks just a small tip like that will make me convinced to join- but It could be true conflict covers my face as I think this ‘if it true then it to big of an opportunity to miss’ I sit up with this thought “and if it's wrong I guess I’ll have just have to deal with it” I walk back into the room to give my answer.

Legacy

Thyus' deeds became legend and secured his place among the Old Six.
