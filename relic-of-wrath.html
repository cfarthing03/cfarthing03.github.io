Original Relic

Relic of Wrath

The relic associated with Wrath before the god fully awakened.

Known Properties

Fashioned from obsidian into the form of a sword, its blade displayed a perpetual, dripping pool of blood. This ancient artifact, infused with the essence of a Dracolich, bestows upon those who form a bond with it the ability to discern their adversaries' vulnerabilities, albeit at the expense of their own mental well-being.
