The Seven Virtues

Diligence

Labor, persistence, discipline, and the refusal to stop simply because the work is ugly.

Diligence is a lycanthropic god of relentless effort: a hulking, scarred, fully furred monster wrapped in only enough ritual ornament to mark him as divine.

Work Without Glamour

Diligence is not a god of effortless mastery. His domain is repetition: the hundredth attempt, the task no one applauds, the wound reopened because the work still is not finished. Progress matters precisely because it costs something.

The Beast Beneath the Halo

His god form rejects elegance. Diligence remains unmistakably lycanthropic—furred, scarred, oversized, and predatory. Chains, ritual cloth, metalwork, and a halo do not civilize the monster. They make it holy.

The Promise of Effort

Followers commonly teach that ability is less sacred than willingness. Talent may open a door, but work decides what survives on the other side. Training, craftsmanship, rebuilding, and long projects are natural acts of devotion.

When Effort Devours Everything

Diligence can become merciless when effort is treated as the answer to every problem. Rest becomes weakness. Injury becomes an excuse. People become valuable only for what they produce. His religion is most frightening when it cannot recognize that stopping can also be necessary.
