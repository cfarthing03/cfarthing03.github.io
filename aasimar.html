Aasimar

Aasimar carry the mark of angels, and in Sinners that is rarely comforting. Celestial lineage is not read as pure or benevolent here. It is read as dangerous, cold, and often as a sign that something fundamentally wrong has entered the room.

Celestial lineageIll omenFeared by many

Connection to Angels

Aasimar are tied to angels by blood, blessing, or some deeper spiritual inheritance. That connection is impossible to separate from their reputation. Wings, halos, radiant marks, and unearthly beauty do not calm people in this world. They unsettle them.

Why They Are Feared

The moral framework of Sinners turns many old assumptions inside out. What another world would call holy, this one often treats with suspicion. Angels are not symbols of safety here, so their descendants are not welcomed as saviors. An aasimar may be seen as cruel before he is known, dangerous before he acts, and guilty before he speaks.

Living Under the Reputation

Some aasimar embrace the fear they inspire. Others try to soften it and spend their whole lives learning that first impressions are not easily undone. Even a kind aasimar often finds that his presence tightens a room. People look at him, and then they look for the nearest exit.

Place in the World

Aasimar are rarer than many other lineages, and that rarity only deepens the mystique around them. Stories about them travel faster than the truth. Depending on the teller, an aasimar is a judge, a monster, a herald, or the shadow of an angel made flesh.
