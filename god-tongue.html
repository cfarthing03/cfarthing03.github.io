God Tongue

No spoken transcription is preserved here.

Unknown origin

Its origin is unknown. Tradition calls it the language of the gods, the words through which they invoke their magic. Whether that explanation is true remains uncertain. Its power does not.

Written exposure

Reading a book containing God Tongue can cause the world itself to shake. Silence does not make the text harmless.

Mortal speech

A mortal who speaks it rapidly loses life force. Blood issues from the eyes and mouth. Even a person of exceptional will may manage only one or two words.

Seven words

The longest mortal utterance is said to have contained seven words: the true names of the gods. The names are not reproduced in this record.
