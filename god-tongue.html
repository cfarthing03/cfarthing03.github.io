Forbidden Language

God Tongue

An ancient language so dangerous that learning, reading, or speaking it can destroy the person attempting it and sometimes far more than the person.

Do Not Read Aloud

God Tongue, the ancient language lost to time, is an enigmatic and perilous linguistic enigma that carries an air of divine mystery. Said to possess immense power and cosmic secrets, the very act of attempting to learn this language is fraught with danger, often resulting in catastrophic consequences. It is whispered that those who dare to delve into the forbidden realms of God Tongue do so at the risk of their own lives and the well-being of the world around them. Within the ancient texts and oral traditions, tales abound of would-be scholars and intrepid explorers who, driven by an insatiable thirst for knowledge, sought to unravel the profound intricacies of God Tongue. However, their journeys were met with disastrous outcomes. As if invoking the wrath of some unseen celestial force, learning the language led to unimaginable calamities and natural disasters, leaving devastation in its wake.

The perils of God Tongue extend beyond its initial acquisition. Even the act of merely reading the language is said to inflict unfathomable torment upon the individual. Those who dare to decipher its cryptic symbols and decipher its ethereal grammar are met with the harrowing consequence of bleeding eyes, a physical manifestation of the intense strain placed upon the mind and body. The very act of comprehending the language exacts a toll that few can endure.

Legends and cautionary tales are rife with accounts of scholars driven to the brink of madness by their encounters with God Tongue. They speak of sleepless nights haunted by cryptic whispers, relentless nightmares that seep into the waking world, and the persistent feeling of being watched by unseen forces. The weight of this forbidden knowledge becomes an unbearable burden, pushing the boundaries of the human psyche to its limits and beyond.

The origins and true nature of God Tongue remain shrouded in obscurity. Some believe it to be the sacred language spoken by the gods themselves, an arcane channel through which they communicated and shaped the very fabric of existence. Others speculate that it holds the keys to unlocking the fundamental mysteries of the universe, concealed within its impenetrable syntax.

Whatever the truth may be, the profound dangers associated with God Tongue have rendered it an alluring yet forbidden realm of knowledge. It serves as a poignant reminder of the limits of human understanding and the unfathomable consequences that await those who dare to challenge them. As the ancient language of immense power lies dormant in the annals of history, its secrets and terrors continue to lure the intrepid few, beckoning them towards an unknown destiny, a destiny fraught with peril and the potential for cataclysmic consequences.
