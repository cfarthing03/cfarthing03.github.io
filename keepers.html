The Keepers

The religious order of Greed and the specialists who keep promises from becoming suggestions.

The Keepers of Contract

The Keepers are the primary religious order devoted to Greed and some of the most accomplished practitioners of Contract Magic in the world. Their faith revolves around ownership, exchange, obligation, ambition, and the power created when people willingly bind themselves to an agreement.

Not every person can create a magical contract. Those with the necessary affinity are therefore valuable, and many Keepers spend their lives writing agreements for people, families, businesses, and governments that cannot create them on their own.

Words Matter

A magical contract enforces what was actually agreed to, not what someone later wishes they had meant. For that reason, Keepers are obsessed with precise language. Loopholes, vague wording, contradictory terms, and badly chosen consequences can turn an ordinary agreement into a disaster.

This gives the order a reputation for being painfully thorough. In their defense, a misplaced sentence can sometimes have significantly worse consequences than an awkward meeting.

Enforcement Is Not Fairness

Contract Magic does not judge morality. It enforces the terms that were willingly accepted. A contract can be generous, balanced, exploitative, or brutally one-sided. The magic itself does not care.

Because of that, many Keepers also work as advisers and mediators. Their responsibility is not only to create a binding contract but to make sure the people signing it understand exactly what they are agreeing to.

Hilnem

Hilnem is built around this magic. Businesses and families such as the Vales provide contract services throughout the city, and some buildings establish magical terms simply by allowing someone to enter. Breaking those terms can trigger whatever consequence was established in the agreement.

The result is one of the safest cities on the continent, provided you develop the very Greed habit of reading before you sign.
