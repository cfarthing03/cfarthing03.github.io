Person of Note

The Inventor

The engineer behind Pride’s mass robot army, and later one of the very people willing to help destroy the master he had once served.

In Pride’s Service

The Inventor once worked for Pride. His defining work during that period was not a single machine, but the creation of Pride’s mass robot army.

That achievement tied his name directly to the machinery Pride could field against the world. Whatever else The Inventor may have been, his work gave Pride an army.

The Break

At some point, The Inventor wanted nothing more to do with Pride. The surviving record does not dress that decision in ceremony. It simply marks the separation: a former servant and creator of Pride’s forces no longer wished to stand with him.

That break became important when The Inventor was introduced to the party.

Turning Against His Former Master

The party intended to kill Pride. The Inventor agreed to help them.

There is a brutal symmetry to the record. The man who had helped make Pride’s army possible ultimately aided the people trying to end Pride himself. The same knowledge that had once strengthened Pride became part of the effort to destroy him.
