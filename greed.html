Greed

Ambition, ownership, prosperity, exchange - and the refusal to let impermanence decide what must be lost.

Greed was the last of the relic-born Sins to awaken. Because his awakening came so late, his immortality was never complete. Rather than accept that weakness, Greed created his own answer to it.

Confidential Divine Nomenclature Agreement

Instrument 7-N / Consideration acknowledged upon execution / Revocable only by page departure

THIS CONFIDENTIAL DIVINE NOMENCLATURE AGREEMENT (the “Agreement”) is entered into voluntarily, knowingly, and with whatever degree of good judgment remains available to the undersigned individual identified below (the “Receiving Party”), in favor of the office, estate, interests, agents, successors, assigns, manifestations, reconstructed bodies, contractual proxies, and continuing interpretive will of Greed (collectively, the “Disclosing Party”).

§ I. Petition for Limited Disclosure

Whereas the Receiving Party has sought knowledge not ordinarily placed within public archives, common scripture, tavern gossip, campaign summaries, player-facing reference materials, or any other source intended to be conveniently searchable; and whereas the Disclosing Party possesses, claims to possess, or is willing to behave as though it possesses such knowledge, the Receiving Party hereby petitions for access to exactly one (1) protected divine designation.

Nothing herein shall be interpreted as a promise that the disclosed designation belongs to Greed specifically. The subject of disclosure shall be selected from the protected inventory according to procedures known to the Disclosing Party and need not correspond to the Receiving Party’s expectations, preferences, theories, favorite Sin, or prior guesses.

§ II. Confidentiality and Non-Distribution

The Receiving Party shall not intentionally disclose, recite, publish, transmit, whisper, gesture, encode, pantomime, screenshot for distribution, place in a group chat, write upon a whiteboard, conceal inside an acrostic, or otherwise communicate the protected designation to any person who has not independently acquired said designation through an authorized lore source.

For avoidance of doubt, “they would probably figure it out anyway,” “I only gave them the first letter,” “I technically said it out of character,” and “I was making a joke” shall not constitute recognized exceptions.

§ III. Separation of Player Knowledge and Character Knowledge

The Receiving Party acknowledges that possession of information by a player does not, by itself, establish possession of that information by any character controlled by said player. The protected designation shall not be invoked, referenced, weaponized, researched with suspicious efficiency, used to bypass investigation, or otherwise imported into play unless the relevant character has independently earned such knowledge within the fiction.

Any attempt to arrange circumstances so that a character “just happens” to guess the correct designation shall be considered metagaming with additional paperwork.

§ IV. Consideration

In consideration for the disclosure, the Receiving Party grants the Disclosing Party one valid signature, the satisfaction of having made somebody read all of this, and the intangible but nevertheless valuable knowledge that the Receiving Party agreed to terms before learning whether the information was useful.

No coin is presently required. The absence of an immediate monetary charge shall not be construed as generosity.

§ V. Accuracy, Completeness, and Dangerous Assumptions

The disclosed designation shall be treated as privileged information within the current session. The Disclosing Party makes no warranty that the Receiving Party understands its meaning, pronunciation, metaphysical weight, historical context, theological implications, or survivability when spoken aloud.

The Receiving Party is specifically advised that a true name is not necessarily a password, command phrase, summoning instruction, conversational icebreaker, or thing one should yell at a god to see what happens.

§ VI. Successive Disclosures

Following execution of this Agreement, the Receiving Party may petition for additional disclosures by executing Supplemental Disclosure Addenda. Each addendum shall authorize no more than one (1) additional protected designation. No designation shall be knowingly repeated until the available protected inventory has been exhausted during the present visit.

The original signature may be carried forward onto subsequent addenda for convenience, because requiring the same person to retype the same name seven times would be inefficient even by bureaucratic standards.

§ VII. Session, Memory, and Revocation

This Agreement and all disclosures issued under it exist only for the duration of the present page session. Navigating away from this record, closing it, or refreshing the page terminates the active filing. Upon return, the Disclosing Party may reorder the protected inventory without notice.

The Receiving Party’s own memory is, regrettably, outside the technical jurisdiction of this document. The confidentiality obligations are not.

§ VIII. Breach

Breach includes direct disclosure, indirect disclosure, deliberate metagaming, “accidental” public speculation performed with suspicious confidence, and any clever interpretation of these clauses whose primary purpose is to avoid the obvious intent of these clauses.

Remedies may include revocation of access, disappointment, additional contractual language, divine side-eye, or such other relief as Greed considers amusing and proportionate.

§ IX. Entire Agreement

This instrument constitutes the complete agreement governing the present disclosure and supersedes any prior promise, assumption, vibe, handshake, nod, wink, or statement beginning with “I thought you meant...” concerning the protected designation.

By signing below, the Receiving Party represents that they have either read this Agreement in full or scrolled rapidly enough to accept the consequences.

The undersigned Receiving Party, whose prior signature remains on file and has been reproduced below for administrative convenience, hereby petitions for disclosure of one (1) additional protected divine designation.

§ A. Incorporation of Prior Terms

Every covenant, restriction, warning, prohibition, disclaimer, confidentiality requirement, anti-metagaming provision, and needlessly specific example contained in the original Agreement is incorporated herein by reference as though copied again in its entirety.

§ B. Non-Repetition

The Disclosing Party shall draw the present disclosure from the protected inventory remaining in this session. A designation already disclosed during the same session shall not be issued again until all seven available designations have been exhausted.

§ C. Continued Separation of Knowledge

The Receiving Party again acknowledges that learning an additional name does not cause any player character to spontaneously remember forbidden theology, discover a convenient ancestral diary, receive an unexplained prophetic dream, or become unusually talented at guessing.

§ D. Continuing Confidentiality

The additional designation is subject to the same restrictions on sharing, publication, hints, coded messages, suspicious facial expressions, and “totally hypothetical” conversations as every prior disclosure.

§ E. Administrative Efficiency

The prior signatory name has been retained solely for execution of subsequent addenda during this visit. It is not stored beyond the current page session. Greed considers this both efficient and, for once, free.

§ F. Execution

Execution of this addendum authorizes exactly one additional disclosure. The Receiving Party may repeat this process until the protected inventory has been exhausted, at which point further petitions shall be denied until a new session begins.

The Last to Awaken

Greed was the last of the relic-born Sins to awaken. Unlike Pride, whose earliest history began as a mortal, Greed did not begin life as a man and did not ascend through some exceptional mortal talent. He emerged from the same ancient relic-born lineage as the other Sins, simply later than the rest.

That delay mattered. By the time Greed fully awakened, he never received the same complete immortality enjoyed by the others. His body could endure for ages, but eventually it would fail. Greed responded exactly as Greed would: he refused to accept the limitation.

An Immortality of His Own

Greed created his own form of immortality. Every few thousand years, his body reaches the end of its cycle and separates into its component pieces. For a time, Greed is no longer whole. Eventually those pieces are drawn back together, reconnecting until Greed reforms and continues on.

This cycle is the source of Greed's strange relationship with mortality. He can fall apart. He can disappear. He can spend ages divided. But the separation is not the same as a permanent death. Greed made sure that, eventually, what belonged together would return to itself.

Contract Magic

Contract Magic is one of the powers most closely associated with Greed and one of the defining forces of Hilnem. A magical contract can enforce terms willingly accepted by its participants and apply whatever consequences were written into the agreement. Some people possess a natural affinity for creating these contracts; others rely on specialists, businesses, and families such as the Vales.

The magic enforces an agreement. It does not decide whether that agreement is fair. A badly written contract can be harsh, exploitative, full of loopholes, or simply disastrous for someone who did not bother to read it. In Hilnem, precision is not just professionalism. It can be self-preservation.

What Greed Means Now

Modern worship does not treat wealth or desire as inherently shameful. Greed represents ambition, the right to own what you have earned, the value of exchange, and the responsibility created when one person promises something to another. Wanting more can build a business, improve a family, or create an entire city. The question is what you are willing to trade to get it.

Hilnem reflects that philosophy. Its contracts make it one of the safest places on the continent, sometimes to absurd degrees. There are buildings where simply walking through the door means agreeing not to commit a crime inside. Greed's city trusts rules because, unlike people, the rules can be made impossible to ignore.
