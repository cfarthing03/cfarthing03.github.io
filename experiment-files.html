Experiment Files

Recovered records concerning Corrective Crystals, test subjects, and procedures never intended for public review.

Test Subject 001 - Ex 239. Experiment 239, Interaction between Corrective Crystal and broken limb We began the experiment by breaking Subject 001’s arm. Though they cried for help the matter went along smoothly with only 3 guards being used to hold them down. After the limb was fractured we attempted to use the crystal to repair it. This was a failure. Since Subject 1 fought, the committee decided not to repair 001’s arm, and not provide any medical support. Will continue experiments tomorrow. At the bottom of the page reads Subject 001’s status Status: Terminated - Means, Alteration

Test Subject 035 - Ex 469 Experiment 469, Interaction between Corrective Crystal and Magic For today's test we had Subject 035 try to cast a simple mage hand spell, while the spell was active we used the corrective crystal. This seems to have agitated the magic as it quickly lashed out towards the user, scarring their hand and making it resemble almost rotting flesh. The subject could no longer control their own magic even when instructed to do so. The committee sees no more use for the test subject so the Alteration has been scheduled. Hopefully, they’ll be of more use then.

Status: Terminated - Means, Alteration Test Subject 046 - Ex 782 Experiment 782, Interaction between Corrective Crystal and Alterations Subject 046 was given a corrective crystal and was instructed to use it on Alteration previously known as 035. It took some convincing however once subject 046 was told that this could possibly bring back 035 they were more than ready to hop into Alteration 035’s containment cell. This may be due to their previous relationship before the Alteration. Subject 046 was once again instructed to use the crystal on the Alteration once they were in the cell. However, this was shortlived as 046 was very quickly torn limb from limb. The committee has instructed a resurrection followed by an Alteration Status: Terminated - Means, Alteration

Test Subject 063 - Ex 1236 Experiment 1236, Interaction between Native Cursed Magic and Correction Crystal Subject 063, was born with Cursed magic which has now been designated as CM - Pathway Pathway allows for a new type of magic that reflects the current user's state of being onto another. It also allows them to manipulate any part of their own body into a sort of weapon. This magic is limited however by the subject still feeling pain making them hesitant to use it. The committee has granted approval for us to begin a new process dubbed “Correction” to alter CM - Pathway and stop the user from feeling any pain. Status: Success, - The user no longer feels any pain and can use the magic freely this, however, has seemed to have suppressed the user's self-preservation, and will end up terminating themselves if not looked after. Additional guards have been supplied to Subjects 063’s cell as they seem too eager to use their newfound abilities. Termination may be necessary. However, this is finally a step in the right direction. Will order more subjects soon

The rest of the paper seems to be covered in blood
