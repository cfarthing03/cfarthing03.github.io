COMMITTEE COPY

TEST SUBJECT 001 / EXPERIMENT 239

Corrective Crystal / Broken Limb

The experiment began by breaking Subject 001's arm. The subject resisted throughout the procedure and required three guards to remain restrained.

Once the fracture was confirmed, a Corrective Crystal was applied to the damaged limb. The procedure failed.

Because Subject 001 continued to struggle, the committee determined that repair of the arm was unnecessary. No medical support was authorized. Testing was scheduled to continue the following morning.

STATUS: TERMINATED
MEANS: ALTERATION

Resistance continues to waste time and personnel.

TEST SUBJECT 035 / EXPERIMENT 469

Corrective Crystal / Active Magic

Subject 035 was instructed to maintain a simple Mage Hand while the Corrective Crystal was introduced to the active spell.

The crystal agitated the magic immediately. The spell recoiled toward the subject, producing severe damage to the casting hand. Tissue blackened and softened within minutes, visually resembling advanced decomposition.

Following exposure, Subject 035 was no longer able to reliably control their own magic even when directly instructed.

The committee determined that the subject no longer possessed sufficient experimental value in their present condition.

STATUS: TERMINATED
MEANS: ALTERATION

Perhaps they will be more useful afterward.

TEST SUBJECT 046 / EXPERIMENT 782

Corrective Crystal / Alteration Interaction

Subject 046 was issued a Corrective Crystal and instructed to enter the containment cell of the Alteration previously designated Subject 035.

Initial compliance was poor. Subject 046 became cooperative after being told the procedure could potentially restore 035. Records indicate a prior relationship between the two subjects.

Once inside the cell, Subject 046 was again instructed to use the crystal.

The test ended before contact could be established. Alteration 035 dismembered Subject 046 within seconds. Recovery staff recorded separation of both arms, the left leg, and extensive removal of abdominal tissue.

The committee approved resurrection followed by Alteration.

STATUS: TERMINATED
MEANS: ALTERATION

Emotional attachment remains useful for compliance.

APPROVED CONTINUATION

TEST SUBJECT 063 / EXPERIMENT 1,236

Native Cursed Magic / Corrective Crystal

Subject 063 possesses a native cursed magic now designated CM — Pathway.

Pathway allows the user to reflect aspects of their current physical state onto another target and reshape portions of their own body into functional weapons. Practical use is limited by pain response and the subject's resulting hesitation.

The committee authorized a new procedure, designated Correction, intended to remove the pain response while preserving the magic's other functions.

The procedure succeeded.

Subject 063 no longer demonstrates measurable pain response and uses Pathway without hesitation. Self-preservation appears to have been suppressed alongside pain. The subject repeatedly inflicts damage that would be fatal without intervention and must now be watched continuously.

Additional guards have been assigned to the cell.

STATUS: SUCCESS
DISPOSITION: CONTINUED OBSERVATION

Finally. A step in the correct direction. Order additional subjects.
