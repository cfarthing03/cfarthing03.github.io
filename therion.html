Therion

A player character remembered through Overgod's Revenge.

Known History

‘It was just supposed to be a simple hunting mission’. I think as lay there on the ground my guts spilled all over the forest floor caused by a stray slash. The creature that did this to me lying dead 20 feat away from me “heh at least i got you as well” i say even as i go though the worst pain ive ever felt. As I look up at the sky my vision darkening i think ‘I’m sorry Tess but your brother ain’t coming back from this one’ before closing my eyes for one last time i see a flash of a weird grayish grandfather clock before everything fades….

Its weird just staring at eternal darkness, everything looks super far away and really close at the same time. Thats what I tought before realizing I can still feel my eyelids actually I could still feel everything. ‘Wait how am i alive’ i think before finally opening my eyes again. As i look around at everything it all looked huge and there where huge bars surrounding me. Its when i try to get up and get to those bars where i realise my first major problem ‘WHY AM I A CHILD!?’ thats when the person right next to me got up from there bed notice I was awake and started walking towards me. Now.. in the time it took me to realise that This was probably my mother and that my head felt funny she was already by the crib. She was 5’6ish with short violet hair that was swept to the side and almost bright velvet colored eyes with a face that was soft but also ruff on the edges. ‘She looks like tess’ is what i thought before she started talking “aw Mamoru now not the time to be up” she said before picking me before bobbing me up and down in her arms “are you thirsty.. Hungry come on let- ah” she said before realising something “ah they already appeared you were quicker than your mother by a year” she said as she look down at me as look up her confused. As shes smiling down at me I here a faint popping noise before two fox like ears appear on her head thats when i realised why my head felt so weird. ‘Im a fox?’ is what i think before passing out again.

Legacy

Therion became a hero to the Automatons, remembered as proof that an Automaton could possess conviction, morality, courage, and the freedom to choose what kind of being they wished to become.
