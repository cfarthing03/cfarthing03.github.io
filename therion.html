Legacy

By the end of his journey, Therion had accomplished everything he believed he needed to accomplish.

The problems surrounding those he cared about had been resolved. The great conflicts that had defined his life had finally come to an end.

And for perhaps the first time, Therion had nothing left that he felt needed fixing.

With a look of quiet satisfaction, he traveled alone to a distant forest, far away from civilization and anyone who might follow.

There, Therion chose to end his own life.

Yet his death did little to diminish what he had become to others.

Though Therion was not originally an Automaton, he became one of their greatest symbols. During the conflict with the Angels, he was the Automaton who stood against them when others could not, openly defying beings that had treated countless lives as disposable.

As more Automatons gained true sentience, Therion's story spread among them.

To many, he became proof that an Automaton could possess not simply thought, but conviction, morality, courage, and the ability to choose what kind of being they wished to become.

Statues, stories, and traditions dedicated to Therion can now be found among Automaton communities.

They do not simply mourn him.

They thank him for having existed.

To the Automatons, Therion was not merely one of their heroes.

He was one of the people who proved that they could have heroes at all.
