Sinners City Pages V16

The opening box on every major city page has been rewritten as direct read-aloud narration for a DM.

Goals of this pass:
- Speak directly to the players using "you".
- No DM-facing phrasing like "your players notice".
- Much longer, richer scene descriptions.
- Emphasis on minute sensory details, movement, architecture, sound, texture, crowd behavior, and environmental clues.
- Avoid telling the audience how to feel. The atmosphere is created through concrete description.
- Preserve all established lore and prior corrections.
- No em dashes anywhere.
