Will

A player character remembered through Tales of the Old Six.

Known History

A man who said he'd make it to a game < 3 hours before the game started, then when the game started, and he wasn't showing up, we made an effort to contact him. He replied by saying that he can't make it to the session anymore because he was in the middle of doing ffxiv maps, then continued to play ffxiv through the 3-4 hour session while complaining we didn't ping him an hour before the session, saying we were "too late"

FFXIV maps are quick 1-5 minute dungeons that you can pick up and stop between at no consequence. He ended up pissing the DM off so his character tripped down the stairs and broke his skull open allowing his Cthulhu therapist to show up and consume him. Books and legends have forgotten he existed

Legacy

History itself appears to have forgotten Will almost completely.
