Dandi

A player character remembered through Tales of the Old Six.

Known History

The surviving archive currently preserves Dandi primarily through the legacy of the Old Six. More of Dandi's personal record has yet to be restored.

Legacy

Dandi's adventures eventually passed into legend.

Their name survived long after their time, and they are now remembered as one of the legendary heroes known as the Old Six.
