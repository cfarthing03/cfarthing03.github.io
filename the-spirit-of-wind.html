Person of Note

The Spirit of Wind

A fragment of Greed who took the form of a woman, crossed the world through the freedom of flight, and ultimately surrendered that freedom when Greed returned.

One of Greed’s Five Pieces

The Spirit of Wind is one of the five pieces of Greed. She takes on the presence of a woman and carries the freedom of flight as the defining expression of what she is.

Unlike the Serpent, her story is not remembered through hostility. It is remembered through movement.

Freedom

She flies across the world, enjoying the freedom that flight gives her. Distance is not a wall in the same way it is for everyone bound to roads, ships, and borders.

For the Spirit of Wind, freedom is not an abstract idea. It is the ability to move through the world without being held to the ground.

Greed’s Return

Eventually, Greed returned. With that return came the end of the separation that had allowed the fragments to exist as independent pieces.

The Spirit of Wind gave up the freedom she loved and returned to Greed. Her story is therefore one of surrender rather than defeat: a fragment that had known the entire world as open sky relinquishing that independence when the whole returned.
