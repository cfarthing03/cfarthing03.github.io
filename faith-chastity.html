Religion of Chastity

The Faith of Chastity

The organized worship of Chastity, built around devotion, boundaries, purity of intent, self-command.

Core Teaching

Followers elevate chosen boundaries, vows, exclusivity, and purity of intent, treating consent and deliberate devotion as sacred lines.

Ordinary Worship

The faith does not require every follower to become clergy. Common devotion is expressed through vows, boundary rites, purification, private devotion. The act matters most when it reflects deliberate commitment rather than public performance.

Temples and Clergy

Temples of Chastity use the same visual language seen in the god's iconography: webs, eyes, black wings, threads, and sealed circles. Clergy are expected to embody the virtue publicly, which makes failures of doctrine especially politically and spiritually significant.

Miracles and Divine Favor

Miracles attributed to Chastity tend to be interpreted through the virtue itself. A believer does not merely ask for power; they ask for the ability to become more completely aligned with Chastity's ideal.

The Dangerous Edge

Its harshest sects can police bodies, relationships, and thought while claiming that externally imposed restriction is the same thing as a chosen boundary.
