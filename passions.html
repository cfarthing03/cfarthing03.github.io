Religion of Lust

The Passions

A faith of love, desire, connection, kindness, and the freedom to choose the bonds that matter.

Love in Every Form

The Passions are the primary religious followers of Lust. Their faith treats love as something far broader than romance. Friendship, family, community, affection, creativity, intimacy, and the desire to build a life with other people can all be expressions of Lust's domain.

The order is likely one of the largest religious communities on the continent. Their temples are often large, well maintained, and heavily involved in everyday community life.

Choice and Consent

Choice is one of the most important principles in Passion theology. Desire without freedom becomes control, and Lust's history with the Rixel Virus makes that distinction impossible for the faith to ignore. Relationships are expected to be built through consent, honesty, respect, and the willingness to let another person remain fully themselves.

The Passions do not expect people to love perfectly. Relationships fail, people hurt one another, and feelings change. Their emphasis is on learning, repairing harm when possible, and becoming better at caring for the people around you.

The Wandering Sage

Advancement within the Passions is overseen by the Wandering Sage, a religious figure who continually travels between the major cities on foot. The Sage recognizes and promotes members considered worthy of greater responsibility within the order.

Because the journey is made entirely on foot, it can take roughly six years before the Sage returns to the same city. A Passion seeking formal recognition may therefore need patience almost as much as devotion.

Temples and Community

Passion temples commonly host weddings, celebrations, counseling, meals, community gatherings, and other events centered on relationships. They are intended to be lived in rather than visited only for formal worship.

For the Passions, caring for people is not adjacent to religion. It is the religion.
