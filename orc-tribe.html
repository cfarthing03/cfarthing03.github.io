The Orc Tribe

A force of roughly ten thousand orcs whose attempt to destroy Palin became the battle where Lex unlocked Susano and Ashwing died, returned, and became legend.

Ten Thousand Moving South

The force remembered simply as the Orc Tribe numbered roughly ten thousand when it turned toward Palin. Whatever individual lives, rivalries, families, and histories existed inside that number became secondary once the march began. The tribe went berserk with a single purpose: reach Palin and destroy it.

Ten thousand bodies moving with that kind of intent is not a raid. It is a moving disaster. Palin is a city built around combat, but even a city that celebrates violence has to take notice when an army large enough to bury streets under sheer numbers decides it wants the walls gone.

The Battle Outside Palin

The assault became remembered less for what the Orc Tribe intended to do and more for what the defenders became while stopping it. Lex, later remembered as Lex the Watcher, unlocked Susano during the battle. The power manifested as a colossal spectral skeleton, large enough to change the scale of the battlefield the moment it appeared.

At the same time, the warrior who would become known as Ashwing reached a terrifying new threshold of his own power. He learned to bring the sun down upon his enemies. The Tribe had marched toward Palin expecting a city of warriors. Instead, they found themselves facing a battlefield where a spectral giant and falling sunlight had become weapons.

The Death and Rebirth of Ashwing

The battle did not end cleanly for Palin's defenders. Ashwing died there.

Then he returned.

His resurrection transformed the battlefield into something far larger than the site of an old invasion. To the followers who would later build faith around Ashwing, the ground became sacred. This was the place where the future Chosen died and was reborn, the place where destruction and rebirth stopped being doctrine and became an event that had physically happened beneath the open sky.

What the Tribe Left Behind

The Orc Tribe failed to destroy Palin. In a strange way, however, the march permanently changed the city they meant to erase. Their assault became the pressure under which Lex unlocked Susano and Ashwing crossed the boundary between death and return. The Tribe is therefore remembered not only as an invading army, but as the force that accidentally created one of Palin's most important religious sites.

Few invading armies would consider that a victory.
