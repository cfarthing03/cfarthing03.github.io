The Continent of Sin

Hilnem

Greed's Ever-Lasting Contract

The continent's great commercial center, protected and imprisoned by the same system of divine contracts.

A City Made of Agreements

Hilnem is the commercial heart of the Continent of Sin. Merchants travel enormous distances to trade here because agreements in Hilnem are not merely promises. Contract Magic can bind terms with supernatural force, making fraud and betrayal significantly more dangerous than they are anywhere else.

Contracts of Greed

The most famous agreements are Contracts of Greed. Their power is tied to the old arrangement between Greed and Wrath: Greed provides the binding contract, while Wrath supplies the consequence. Breaking a valid Contract of Greed can turn a private dispute into a divine death sentence.

The Contract You Sign by Entering

Visitors agree to Hilnem's laws the moment they enter. The city itself functions like a contract, and individual buildings, businesses, services, and institutions may impose additional terms. Experienced travelers learn quickly to read what they are agreeing to before crossing a threshold.

Safety Through Severity

Hilnem is frequently described as one of the strictest cities on the continent, and not without reason. The same rules that irritate visitors also make many crimes remarkably difficult to sustain. A person can walk through Hilnem carrying extraordinary wealth with a level of confidence that would be suicidal in Hus.

Greed's Return

During the events surrounding the Revolution of Pride, Greed returned from the divine realm to the mortal world. He now resides in Hilnem and continues to provide Contracts of Greed while observing the city's development. Gods rarely live openly among mortals, making Hilnem one of the few places where divine authority is not an abstraction.

Ambition Under Rules

Hilnem does not discourage ambition. It encourages ambition with terms attached. Wealth, power, influence, and opportunity are everywhere, but so are consequences. The city's philosophy is simple: want as much as you like—just understand what you agreed to in order to get it.
