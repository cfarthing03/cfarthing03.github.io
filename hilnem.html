The Continent of Sin

Hilnem

Greed's Ever-Lasting Contract

The continent's great commercial city, where almost every meaningful interaction can become binding.

A City Built on Contracts

Hilnem's society revolves around Contract Magic. Merchants, businesses, institutions, and private citizens use formal agreements constantly, making the city one of the most reliable places in the world to conduct trade.

Contracts of Greed

The city's most famous agreements are Contracts of Greed. Their power is tied to Greed and Wrath, creating consequences far beyond ordinary law. A person who knowingly violates such a contract risks divine punishment rather than merely a lawsuit.

Entering Hilnem

The city's rules begin before most visitors realize it. Entry itself is treated as acceptance of Hilnem's civic contract. Many buildings and services carry their own additional agreements, meaning life in the city rewards careful reading and punishes assumptions.

The Safest City

Hilnem's strictness gives it a reputation for being exhausting, but the same system also makes it unusually safe. Theft, fraud, and betrayal are far harder to sustain when every serious exchange is backed by supernatural consequence.

Greed in the Mortal Realm

Greed has returned to the mortal realm and currently resides within Hilnem. His direct presence has made the city's relationship with contracts even more intense. What was once a society shaped by his legacy is now also a city operating beneath his active gaze.
