Tales Character

Farnear

A player character remembered through Clockwork Resurgence.

Known History

Born and raised in the now destroyed Sol, was a town guard but couldn't keep up with the increasing demand for protection and was removed from his position, since his only real skills were in fighting he went out across the continent looking for work, before he left he became engaged with a beautiful woman by the name of Adeline, they were childhood friends and she refused to let him go off adventuring without her. They continued their journey doing small jobs anywhere they could. Eventually ending up in a town known as Hus. Though the city was not small they found plenty of work, and even more, parties to attend., So they decided to settle there for a while before continuing their journey. This leads them to meet up with the rest of the party.

Legacy

Known in ancient stories as the Son of Sloth, Farnear remained mortal despite his connection to the Sin. Somehow, he possessed the ability to wield fragments of Sloth’s own power, something believed to be nearly impossible for an ordinary mortal.

Exactly how this connection came to be has never been fully understood.

Legends of Farnear exist throughout the lands once influenced by Sloth, though frustratingly little is actually known about the man himself. Accounts describe him as incredibly elusive, rarely remaining in one place for long and seemingly uninterested in fame, worship, or recognition.

Stories occasionally place him at major historical events, often appearing without explanation before disappearing just as quickly.

Because of this, historians disagree on how much of Farnear’s legend is true. Some consider him little more than folklore surrounding Sloth, while others believe Farnear deliberately ensured that very little about his life survived.

Even centuries later, the Son of Sloth remains one of history’s greatest unknowns.
