Tales Character

Eldridge

A player character remembered through Echoes of the Velvet City.

Known History

Eldridge Mikkelson, legally Allard Rolan, survived a childhood inside the Messengers of the Chosen and later tried to build a life as a member of the Imperial Guard. He married Hensley Mikkelson, but unresolved rage toward the cult drove him into a series of killings that eventually expanded beyond those directly responsible for what happened to him. Hensley discovered the truth and forced Eldridge to leave rather than turn him in. Eldridge now lives with the consequences of what he survived and what he chose to become.

Legacy

Eldridge's final place in the history of the world remains unknown. His story is not over.
