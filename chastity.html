The Seven Virtues

Chastity

Devotion, boundaries, purity of intent, and the right to decide what is allowed to reach you.

Chastity is a dark-winged goddess surrounded by eyes, threads, and spiderlike limbs: a divine image of boundaries that can become a cage.

More Than Abstinence

Chastity is not limited to sex. Her broader domain is chosen boundary: what a person permits into their body, mind, relationships, vows, and identity. Devotion has meaning because it excludes what would violate the choice.

The Web

Her imagery uses threads and webs because a boundary can connect as easily as it can divide. Every promise creates a line: this is mine, this is yours, this is shared, and this is forbidden.

Purity of Intent

Her worship emphasizes clarity of purpose. A vow made for status is empty. A boundary enforced only when convenient is not a boundary. Chastity's strictest followers believe integrity requires consistency even when nobody is watching.

When Protection Becomes a Cage

Her virtue turns cruel when purity is defined by someone else. Boundaries can become walls built around another person. Devotion can become isolation. A doctrine meant to defend autonomy can become a reason to police bodies, relationships, and thought.
