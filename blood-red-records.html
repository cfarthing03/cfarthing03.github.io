Blood Red

Working notes. Trial numbers preserved. Names omitted as irrelevant.

SUBJECT 004 / DIRECT CONTACT / FAILURE

The wound accepts it.

A fragment was placed into a fresh incision along the left forearm. Contact with blood produced immediate growth. The lattice followed the wet margins first, then entered the tissue beneath them.

At twelve minutes, the subject reported pressure inside the radius. At nineteen, the skin rose in narrow ridges as growth formed beneath it. At twenty-six, the ridges split. The exposed crystal did not push blood outward. It drank it. Droplets that should have fallen to the table instead crawled back toward the openings in thin red threads.

The subject requested removal at thirty-one minutes. Requests for amputation were entered in the margin. No removal was performed.

Independent arm movement ended at forty-eight minutes. Speech continued for another eleven. The subject repeated their own name until only the first syllable remained.

Useful. The material does not merely grow in blood. It recognizes blood as substrate.

SUBJECT 019 / POSTMORTEM CONTACT / SUCCESS

Death does not interrupt integration.

Cadaveric tissue was expected to be inert. It was not.

The fragment entered through the mouth and rooted along the tongue, gums, and throat before spreading into the chest. The jaw dislocated during expansion. Several teeth were forced inward and remained suspended inside the new growth. The body stood without respiration.

Locomotion was poor. Response to sound and movement was immediate. Damage that would disable a living person produced no meaningful hesitation.

The designation Crystal Zombie is serviceable. They are fragile, simple, and cheap to produce. More important, they carry viable growth into new bodies.

First repeatable organism.

SUBJECT 067 / VOLUME FEEDING / PARTIAL SUCCESS

More blood does not create more of the same.

Continuous feeding changed the growth pattern. The crystal stopped replacing the host and began building around it.

By the third reservoir the lower body could no longer be identified as human. Bone was incorporated wherever it happened to lie. A kneecap surfaced near the sternum. Three fingers remained embedded beneath a clear plate along the back, still bending when pressure was applied to the original wrist.

The subject remained conscious long after the face was obscured. Vocalization continued from somewhere inside the thoracic mass after the mouth had sealed.

Size is therefore not limited by the host. Only by available material.

Increase supply. Stop thinking in terms of one body.

SUBJECT 142 / LIVING FUSION / STABLE

Durability can be selected for.

The host was restrained through the first growth phase to prevent the usual tearing caused by panic. Slower feeding produced denser layering around the torso and limbs.

The finished organism was broad, slow, and almost impossible to redirect once moving. Intelligence degraded quickly. When ordered through a reinforced door, it continued after the obstacle was gone and crossed two additional rooms before striking the exterior wall.

Opening the shoulder after termination revealed the original arm folded inside the crystal shell. The hand had been pressed backward against the humerus until every finger broke. The nails were still intact.

Crystal Tank. Imperfect. Stupid. Excellent.

A mind is optional if the body is useful.

SUBJECT 311 / DIRECTED FUSION / BREAKTHROUGH

Intelligence can survive after the person does not.

Previous trials lost too much cognition. Reduced saturation preserved problem solving while eliminating hesitation, distress response, and self-directed behavior.

The organism understood tools before being shown how to make them. Its right forearm softened, split along the ulna, and reformed into a hooked blade. When the blade dulled, it absorbed the damaged edge and produced another.

Questions concerning name, family, pain, or memory produced no response. Tactical questions were answered immediately.

During dissection, the face beneath the outer plate was found intact. The eyes tracked the room after the surrounding body had stopped moving.

Crystal Soldier. Near-perfect fusion. Intelligence retained. Sentience absent.

This is closer.

SUBJECT 728 / SELF-PROPAGATION / SUCCESS

It can make more of itself.

A long-form body was encouraged through confined feeding channels. The resulting organism preferred enclosed spaces and moved through soil with considerably greater speed than expected.

Most important: separated sections did not die. Given blood, each fragment began rebuilding.

One specimen tunneled beneath the holding block and remained undetected for six hours. It returned with portions of three missing handlers incorporated into a developing secondary mass. One face remained visible from the cheekbones upward. The eyelids had crystallized open. The mouth moved when the primary organism moved, though no sound emerged.

Crystal Serpent. Fast. Cunning. Reproductive. An infestation rather than a soldier.

A city would feed it for months.

SUBJECT 1,906 / PERFECT HUMAN INTEGRATION / SUCCESS

The host can remain inside.

I had assumed personhood and complete fusion were mutually exclusive. Subject 1,906 disproved that.

The crystal consumed the body without destroying continuity of thought. The subject remembered their name, their childhood address, the sequence of tests, and every restraint used on them. They also demonstrated control over nearby lesser organisms without speech or gesture.

When asked how, they said, “They are loud when you know where to listen.”

The subject then ordered three Zombies to dismantle themselves against the observation glass. They obeyed until nothing remained capable of movement.

Crystal Zombie Perfected. Sentience intact. Hive authority confirmed.

Not the end point. Proof of principle.

SUBJECT SERIES 4,880–6,214 / MASS INTEGRATION / UNSTABLE

Many bodies will join. They will not agree.

Individual hosts are an unnecessary constraint. Pooled blood and living material produced a body large enough that internal anatomy became meaningless.

The problem is not growth. The problem is disagreement.

Faces repeatedly formed along the exterior and were pulled back inside. Hands emerged where there were no arms. One cluster of teeth opened and closed along the abdomen for nearly four hours. Voices overlapped from within the mass, some pleading, some screaming, some reciting names, some answering questions asked to other mouths.

When the organism attempted to move, different portions pulled in opposing directions until several embedded bodies separated at the joints. The crystal repaired the tears. The screaming resumed before repair was complete.

Crystal Behemoth. Immense strength. Catastrophic instability. Survival and destruction appear to be the only shared impulses strong enough to coordinate the whole.

Failure, but an informative one. The souls remain. That is the obstacle.

SUBJECT SERIES 23,441+ / MASS INTEGRATION / ONE SUCCESS

Agreement.

The Behemoth failed because thousands of occupants fought for the same body. The solution was not to remove them.

It was to give them one voice.

The successful organism formed without the spasms seen in prior masses. Thousands of faces were visible during early growth, then slowly sank beneath the surface as though lowered under dark water. No restraints were required once the primary consciousness emerged.

It spoke in the singular.

Sections removed from the body continued moving independently, obeyed commands at distance, and returned to rejoin the whole. Damage became temporary. Division became movement.

When asked how many individuals remained inside it, the organism answered, “Enough.”

Crystal Dragon. Perfected mass integration. Sentient. Intelligent. Self-dividing. Self-reforming. One successful creation.

The quantity of blood required is obscene. The number of failed subjects is greater. Neither is a meaningful limitation if the result can be reproduced.

I am close now. I know that I am close.
