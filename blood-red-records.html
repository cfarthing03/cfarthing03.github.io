Blood Red

Recovered working notes. Observations are provisional.

I. Living substrate

SUBJECT L-04 / INITIAL CONTACT

A fragment was introduced into an open wound in the forearm. Growth followed the wet margins before extending beneath the skin. The subject remained responsive. Removal was requested repeatedly.

The first visible branches lifted the skin rather than breaking it. As the lattice thickened, narrow splits formed along the arm. Blood collected in these openings and was absorbed before it reached the table. The subject described movement inside the bone. This statement could not be independently verified.

By the next observation, flexion brought the exposed growths into contact with one another. The resulting sound was recorded alongside continued vocalization. Requests for amputation were entered in the margin. No removal was performed.

The mouth remained capable of speech after independent movement had ceased. The subject continued giving their name. Later responses contained only the first syllable. No reliable point of transition from person to organism has been established.

II. Postmortem integration

Consumption of a corpse produces the common Crystal Zombie. These organisms are individually fragile, occur in substantial numbers, and are the most likely form to transmit further infection. Living fusion must be recorded separately.

III. Blood feeding

Blood stimulates growth. At extreme volumes, the work ceases to resemble integration of a single host. The quantities required for a Behemoth correspond to thousands, potentially hundreds of thousands, of individuals. Such a creature cannot arise naturally. Its production requires deliberate collection.

Faces and hands can be seen within imperfect formations. They press toward the surface without displacing it. Their mouths remain open. The exterior shows no corresponding fracture. The donors have not departed.

IV. Recorded forms

Crystal Zombie Perfected. Extremely rare, perfect human fusion. Sentience remains intact. The individual can control other crystal organisms through an apparent hive mind. Physical strength is an unreliable measure of the threat.

Crystal Tank. Imperfect fusion with a living human. Slow, extremely unintelligent, and highly durable. Useful for breaking formations and disrupting organized resistance.

Crystal Soldier. Near-perfect fusion preserves intelligence but removes sentience. The body can mold weapons, armor, and structures. Slow, calculating, and durable. Its presence indicates a commander.

Crystal Serpent. A fast, cunning burrower intended to infest cities from below. It constructs extensive tunnels, lures victims underground, and reproduces by separating portions of its own body and feeding victims to the new growth. Durability is limited; speed and reproduction compensate.

Crystal Behemoth. An imperfect mass formation concerned only with survival and destruction. Insufficient blood, inadequate incubation, or conflict between resident souls may prevent stabilization. Souls visibly scream and reach through the crystal without escaping.

Crystal Dragon. The perfected mass formation. One consciousness controls a body containing thousands of souls. Fully sentient, intelligent, and exceptionally dangerous. It can separate into independently moving pieces and reform. Cooperation between souls is a possible explanation, not an established mechanism. Only one successful creation is recorded.
