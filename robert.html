Person of Note

Robert

A Hus drug dealer who turned against Life's Blood and eventually bore Gluttony's Sinners Mark.

Archive Record

Robert was once a lonely drug dealer in the streets of Hus. However one day his life would change for ever when a man named Lex the Watcher would reveal the dangerous effects of the drug and what he was bringing upon the poor citizens of Hus. Robert right then and there decided to help Lex in his goal of shutting down the ring of Life's Blood once and for all. With his general knowledge and Lex's fighting ability they became an unstoppable duo. Eventually, Robert obtained the Sinners Mark of Gluttony. Becoming one with The Pantheon.
