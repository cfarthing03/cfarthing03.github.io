Person of Note

Robert

A lonely drug dealer from the streets of Hus who learned what Life’s Blood was doing, chose to turn against it, and eventually carried the Sinners Mark of Gluttony.

A Dealer in Hus

Robert began as a lonely drug dealer in the streets of Hus. His place in the story could easily have ended there, another person moving product through a city already infamous for crime and drugs.

Then Lex the Watcher confronted him with the truth about Life’s Blood and the damage it was bringing down on the citizens of Hus.

The Choice

Robert did not need time to build a heroic identity around what came next. Once Lex showed him the dangerous effects of the drug, Robert decided to help shut the Life’s Blood ring down.

The decision completely changed the direction of his life. Knowledge that had once served him on the street became useful against the very network he had been part of.

Robert and Lex

Robert brought general knowledge of the streets and the drug trade. Lex brought the fighting ability to survive the people who stood in their way.

Together they became an exceptionally effective pair in the effort to dismantle the Life’s Blood operation. Robert’s story stopped being about selling the drug and became about helping destroy the system that moved it.

The Mark of Gluttony

Robert eventually obtained the Sinners Mark of Gluttony. With that, the former dealer from Hus became part of The Pantheon.

The distance between where Robert began and where he ended is what makes his record matter. His life changed because someone showed him what his actions were helping cause, and he chose to act on that knowledge.
