Mythic Origin

The First Sin

Before the Sins were worshiped as gods, there was a mortal Pride, a set of relics, and the beginning of a cycle that would shape the entire world.

The Forging

The oldest surviving account claims that Pride came first, not as a relic-born god, but as a mortal who reached beyond what mortals were meant to hold. In that ambition, Pride forged the relics that would become the foundation of the Sins.

The Waiting Relics

Those relics did not awaken all at once. They existed first as vessels, heavy with possibility and shaped by intention, fear, desire, and belief. Over time, faith and memory gathered around them until the relics began to stir with lives of their own.

The Awakening of the Sins

From those relics came the Sins. Pride stood apart from them because Pride had been human first. The others would wake in their own order, with Sloth rising first among the relic-born powers and Greed waking last. By then the world had already learned the lesson that history keeps repeating: belief does not simply describe power here. It creates it.

Why the Story Matters

This story is remembered as the first true break in the old order. It explains why relics matter, why worship changes the divine, and why so many later conflicts begin with someone trying to control a power they barely understand.
