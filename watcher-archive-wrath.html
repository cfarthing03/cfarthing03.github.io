The Third Voice

By the time Wrath awakened, Pride had stopped treating consciousness as a singular impossibility. Sloth had proven it could happen. Lust had proven it could happen again. The third awakening confirmed a pattern.

Wrath emerged first from relic-state and later into the animal form preserved in the oldest accounts: a lion. As his power increased, that shape gave way to the humanoid forms associated with him in later history.

Pride's concern was not that Wrath could think. It was what Wrath could do with time.

Pride // temporal asset W-03Localized temporal manipulation confirmed. Utility exceeds initial projection. Laboratory throughput may be increased without corresponding external duration.

Secondary observation: W-03 and S-01 produce mutually disruptive effects. Investigate containment applications.

That second observation defined the next period of both their lives.

Two Leashes

Sloth and Wrath's powers opposed one another strongly enough that Pride could use each as a restraint on the other. When Sloth became difficult to control, Wrath was brought in. When Wrath became dangerous, Pride used Sloth.

Pride then made certain they blamed each other for it.

Recovered instructions show altered reports, selectively repeated conversations, and punishments delivered immediately after one Sin was told the other had caused them. Pride did not need them merely separated. He needed them to believe that the person across the room was part of the cage.

Wrath

I thought he chose to hold me down.

He thought I chose to do the same to him.

Pride made sure we never saw the hand holding both chains.

The hatred lasted. Later statements from both Sins describe genuine anger, not a misunderstanding resolved the moment they learned the truth. Pride had given each of them years of memories in which the other's presence meant pain.

Watcher annotationWrath and Sloth eventually agreed that neither had been free to refuse the role. Agreement did not erase what they had done to one another while trapped inside it.

Borrowed Time

Wrath's primary value to Pride was acceleration.

Experiments that should have required months of observation could be given months inside a much shorter span of outside time. Growth cycles, material fatigue, repeated procedures, recovery windows, and prototype revisions could all be advanced while the world beyond the laboratory barely moved.

This is one reason the chronology of Pride's work is unusually difficult to reconstruct. Calendar dates understate how much time the Sins experienced inside controlled temporal fields.

Pride // scheduling memorandumExternal time is no longer a meaningful constraint on development. W-03 can provide sufficient internal duration for repeated iteration provided correction remains available.

Do not permit unsupervised temporal access.

Watcher historians therefore distinguish between calendar duration and experienced duration. Sloth's seventy-five-year correction is one of the few periods whose stated duration appears to refer to experienced time rather than ordinary historical dating.

Correction

Wrath rebelled more than once.

Pride never subjected him to anything approaching Sloth's seventy-five years, but the difference should not be mistaken for mercy. Wrath was restrained, injured, deprived of control over his own time, and forced through procedures Pride labeled correction. The severity was calibrated carefully enough to leave Wrath useful.

Sloth's condition remained visible throughout.

Wrath

I knew there was a line.

I didn't know exactly where it was.

Sloth was what Pride put at the end of it.

Every rebellion therefore carried two calculations: what Wrath believed was right, and whether acting on that belief would turn his punishment into Sloth's.

Watcher theologians consider this the clearest origin point for Wrath's later domains. Pride used the word correction to mean pain imposed until behavior changed. Wrath kept the word and changed what it meant. Correction became the obligation to identify what is wrong, judge what justice requires, and act with enough strength to make that judgment matter.

Post-escape analysis

Justice Is Not Obedience

Wrath's history explains an apparent contradiction in his worship. He is a god of strength who distrusts strength without purpose, a god of correction who knows correction can be abuse, and a god of justice who spent years being told that obedience and justice were the same thing.

Time gave Pride the ability to do more. It eventually gave Wrath the perspective to ask whether more should have been done at all.

Wrath

He taught me punishment first.

Justice took longer.
