Original Relic

Relic of Greed

The relic associated with Greed before the god fully awakened.

Known Properties

The ancient artifact in question is a diminutive cowboy hat adorned with a chain encircling its brim. When donned, this peculiar hat grants the wearer the extraordinary ability to manipulate and manifest chains at their command. Typically employed as a means to immobilize adversaries or traverse between locations with finesse, it can also be wielded as a formidable weapon to inflict harm in desperate situations.
