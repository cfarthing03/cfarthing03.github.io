Religion of Sloth

The Spirits

Rest, resilience, grief, death, rebirth, and the natural rhythm of beginning again.

Followers of Sloth

The Spirits are the primary religious order devoted to Sloth. They tend to value calm judgment, recovery, endurance, grief, sleep, death, and rebirth. Their faith rejects the idea that rest is the opposite of usefulness. Rest is what allows anything living to continue.

Spirits often maintain sanctuaries designed for quiet rather than spectacle, including gardens, groves, meditation spaces, sleeping chambers, and places where people can simply be left alone for a while.

The Trials of Sloth

Those seeking to become formal clerics or paladins of Sloth must undergo the Trials of Sloth. The exact details are kept deliberately mysterious to outsiders, but the Trials are known to be extremely arduous. Screams and pleas for forgiveness have been heard from those undergoing them, which has done very little to make outsiders less curious.

The purpose is not cruelty for cruelty's sake. The Trials test resilience, devotion, endurance, and what remains of a person when comfort, certainty, and control are stripped away.

Death and Transition

The Spirits frequently care for the dying and the grieving. Death is treated as part of a cycle rather than a simple defeat, though that does not mean grief is dismissed or hurried. An ending can be natural and still hurt.

Funerals, mourning, and other transitions therefore occupy an important place in the faith. Healing is allowed to take time.

Sol

Sol reflects Sloth's philosophy through its unusual daily rhythm. Much of the city rests during the day and becomes active at night, when festivals and celebrations fill the streets.

The schedule makes the city's values unusually literal: rest first, then live.
