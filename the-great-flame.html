Person of Note

The Great Flame

A blue-flame fragment of Greed whose impatience helped turn one afternoon in Sol into a catastrophe.

One of Greed's Pieces

The Great Flame was one of the five separated pieces of Greed, taking the form of a destructive blue flame. Unlike the Serpent, the Great Flame was not an enemy of the world. His actions in Sol, however, still became part of one of the city's worst disasters.

The Wielders of Flame

When signs pointed toward Greed's return, the Great Flame gathered followers known as the Wielders of Flame. Their purpose was to locate Vale, Greed's reincarnated human form, and bring him to the Great Flame so that the fragment could fulfill its end of the contract and return to the whole.

Vale did not know what he truly was, and he did not come. The Great Flame grew impatient. That same afternoon, he ordered the Wielders of Flame to raze the town where Vale was believed to be hiding. Sol survived the attack, but only barely.

The Afternoon Sol Fell

The destruction did not end with the Great Flame. Later that same afternoon, the Serpent reached Vale first, bound him through a contract, and finished what the earlier attack had begun. By the end of the day, Sol had been devastated.
