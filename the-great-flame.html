Person of Note

The Great Flame

A blue-flame fragment of Greed whose desire to reunite with the whole became one half of the catastrophe that destroyed Sol in a single afternoon.

One of Greed’s Five Pieces

The Great Flame was one of the five separated pieces of Greed, taking the form of a destructive blue flame. He was not, like the Serpent, an enemy of the world. That distinction does not erase what happened in Sol.

When signs pointed toward Greed’s return, every fragment faced the same approaching truth: the separated pieces would be drawn back toward the whole.

The Wielders of Flame

The Great Flame gathered followers known as the Wielders of Flame. Their purpose was to find Vale, Greed’s reincarnated human form, and bring him to the Great Flame so the fragment could fulfill its end of the contract and return to Greed.

Vale did not know what he truly was. He did not arrive. The longer the search continued, the less patient the Great Flame became.

The Order to Raze the Town

Eventually, impatience became destruction. The Great Flame ordered the Wielders of Flame to raze the town where Vale was believed to be hiding.

The attack devastated Sol. The city survived the first blow, but only barely. Blue fire and the actions of the Wielders became the opening half of a disaster that was not yet finished.

The Afternoon Sol Fell

Later that same afternoon, the Serpent reached Vale before the other fragments could reunite with him. The Serpent bound Vale through a contract and completed the destruction the Great Flame’s followers had begun.

Sol did not fall through a single act. It was broken twice in the same afternoon. The Great Flame helped create the wound. The Serpent made certain the city did not survive it intact.
