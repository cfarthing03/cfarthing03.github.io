Person of Note

The Samurai

A rogue adventurer who wielded the power of yokai, set out to kill Pride himself, and ended as one of the very soldiers serving the enemy he had meant to destroy.

A Rogue Adventurer

The Samurai was a rogue adventurer who harnessed the power of yokai. He did not use that power for a small target. His goal was Pride himself.

The ambition of that mission is part of why his story survives. He was willing to aim directly at one of the most dangerous powers in the world rather than remain at the edges of the conflict.

The Attempt on Pride

The Samurai tried to kill Pride. He failed.

Failure did not simply mean death or retreat. Pride captured him, and what followed was worse because it turned the purpose of his life against itself.

Remade as a Soldier

The Samurai was transformed into one of Pride’s robot soldiers.

The adventurer who had come to end Pride was absorbed into Pride’s forces instead. His record ends on that reversal: a would-be killer of Pride remade into a weapon serving him.
