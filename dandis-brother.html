Person of Note

Dandi's Brother

Dandi’s kidnapped brother became the unwilling host of the Relic of Sloth, and his story ended with a death Dandi begged another member of the party to prevent.

The Kidnapping

Dandi’s brother was taken. That single act pulled him out of the life he had known and into the path of forces far larger than himself.

His importance to the story did not come from a quest for power or a choice to involve himself with the divine. It came from what was done to him after he was already in someone else’s hands.

Host of the Relic of Sloth

He was used as a host for the Relic of Sloth. The relic’s presence made his fate inseparable from one of the powers that would shape the world, but it did not make the situation any less personal for Dandi.

Behind the relic, the danger, and everything surrounding it was still Dandi’s brother.

The Death Dandi Could Not Stop

Another member of the party killed him. Dandi begged them to stop, but the killing happened anyway.

That is the entire cruelty of the record. There is no grand victory hidden inside it. A kidnapped man was made into a host, and when the moment came that his life depended on the people around him, Dandi’s plea was not enough to save him.
