Original Relic

Relic of Sloth

The relic associated with Sloth before the god fully awakened.

Known Properties

The helmet belonged to a formerly indolent knight. When worn, it unveils two imperceptible entities, visible exclusively to the wearer. Firstly, a shield materializes, encircling the user in a clockwise motion, adeptly warding off any harm from that particular side. Secondly, a sword manifests, launching attacks effortlessly without the need for any physical exertion from the user. In the hands of a skilled warrior, both these concealed forces can prove lethal in the heat of battle.
