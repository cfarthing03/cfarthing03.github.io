Person of Note

The Serpent

The antagonistic fragment that represented Greed's body, deceived Vale, and finished the destruction of Sol.

The Body of Greed

The Serpent was one of Greed's five separated pieces and represented Greed's body. Among those fragments, the Serpent alone became an antagonist.

Vale

When Greed returned through the mortal reincarnation known as Vale, Vale did not understand what he was. The Serpent exploited that ignorance and reached him before the other fragments could reunite with him.

The Fall of Sol

Earlier that same afternoon, the Great Flame's followers had already razed the town where Vale was believed to be hiding. The Serpent then completed Sol's destruction. The city that had endured the Wielders of Flame did not survive the Serpent.
