Person of Note

The Serpent

The fragment that represented Greed’s body, found Vale before the others could reach him, exploited what Vale did not know, and finished the destruction of Sol.

The Body of Greed

The Serpent was one of Greed’s five separated pieces and represented Greed’s body. Among those fragments, the Serpent alone became an antagonist.

That distinction matters because the pieces were not identical copies of Greed. They carried different portions of the whole, and the Serpent chose a path the others did not.

Vale

Greed returned through the mortal reincarnation known as Vale. Vale did not know what he truly was.

The Serpent exploited that ignorance and reached him before the other fragments could reunite with him. That gave the Serpent access to a man who did not yet understand the divine identity gathering around him.

A Contract Before Reunion

The Serpent bound Vale through a contract. By reaching him first, the Serpent was able to shape what happened before the other pieces could restore Greed in the way they intended.

Vale’s ignorance made that possible. The person at the center of Greed’s return was also the person least equipped to understand what the fragments wanted from him.

The Fall of Sol

Earlier that same afternoon, the Great Flame had already ordered the Wielders of Flame to raze the town where Vale was believed to be hiding. Sol survived that attack, but only barely.

The Serpent then completed the destruction. The city had already been devastated when the second blow arrived. By the end of the afternoon, what the Great Flame had begun, the Serpent had finished.
