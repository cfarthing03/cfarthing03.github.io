Faction

Dragon's Tail

A mercenary company willing to take almost any work for the right price, with loyalty measured in contracts rather than causes.

Mercenaries Without a Cause

Dragon's Tail is a mercenary company built around a simple promise: if the price is right, the job is worth discussing. They are not a knightly order, a church militia, or a band of heroes pretending coin has nothing to do with why they arrived. Dragon's Tail sells capability. Their reputation comes from being willing to take work that respectable organizations would refuse to touch, especially when the client needs people who will not spend the first hour asking whether the assignment is noble.

That does not mean the company is mindless. Their willingness to work for almost anyone is precisely why clients seek them out. The value of Dragon's Tail is not that they are reckless. It is that they are professional enough to treat an ugly contract like any other contract. Escort work, recovery, monster hunting, intimidation, protection, sabotage, and stranger requests all fall under the same rule. If the company believes the job can be done and the client can meet the price, morality is rarely the first obstacle.

The Price Comes First

Dragon's Tail has never pretended loyalty is free. The group is loyal to the agreement made with a client and to the payment attached to it. That makes them easy to understand and difficult to trust in equal measure. A city guard serves a city. A church serves a god. Dragon's Tail serves the terms of the current job.

This has made the company useful to people who cannot afford questions and dangerous to people who assume reputation and friendship mean the same thing. Someone who hired Dragon's Tail once may find them perfectly pleasant the next time they meet. They may also find them standing on the opposite side of a road because someone else paid first.

Why People Still Hire Them

The obvious question is why anyone would rely on mercenaries so openly motivated by coin. The answer is that Dragon's Tail does not hide what it is. There is comfort in dealing with an organization whose motivations are visible. They do not need to believe in you. They do not need to like you. They need a clear assignment, an acceptable price, and enough reason to believe the contract is worth taking.

For many clients, that honesty is worth more than virtue. Dragon's Tail earned its place in the continent's underbelly because there will always be work that governments, churches, and celebrated adventurers will not accept. Someone still has to do it.

Common saying: If Dragon's Tail turned you down, either you could not pay enough or the job was worse than you thought.
