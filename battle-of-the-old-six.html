Historic Battle

Battle of the Old Six

The Old Six are remembered as adventurers, but legends rarely survive unless there was a field where everything could have ended and somehow did not.

A Battle Larger Than Its Record

The surviving histories do not agree on every detail of the battle. Names, locations, and even the exact cause shift depending on who tells it. What never changes is the result. The Old Six entered a fight that should have crushed them and came out as something larger than ordinary heroes.

What Is Remembered Clearly

922, Dandi, Ferrithorn, Thyus, Xan Mera, and Will are all bound to this memory. Whether the battle was against an army, a relic-fueled threat, or a disaster no one else could stop, it became the moment that fixed them in the world’s imagination.

Why It Endured

The battle matters because it turned a party into a standard that later generations would be measured against. Later heroes inherited their consequences, their unfinished stories, and in some cases even their titles. The Old Six stopped being people in the mouths of most storytellers. They became a benchmark for legend.

The Problem With Legends

As with many events in Sinners, belief makes the memory heavier. The more the battle is retold, the more it begins to feel less like a simple old fight and more like a foundational truth. That makes it powerful, but it also makes it difficult to separate fact from the shape faith has carved around it.
