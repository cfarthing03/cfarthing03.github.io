Historic Conflict

Battle of the Old Six

Some battles are remembered because kingdoms fall. This one is remembered because six people walked into something they were never meant to survive and came back out legendary.

Before the Name Became Sacred

When the battle began, the Old Six were not yet the Old Six. They were adventurers, powerful certainly, but still flesh and doubt and bad decisions wrapped in mortal skin. The names most often tied to the company are 922, Dandi, Ferrithorn, Thyus, Xan Mera, and Will. Later centuries would speak those names with the distance usually reserved for myth, but on the day of the battle they were simply the six who stood there.

That detail is important. Legends become less frightening when people forget they started as people. The Old Six were not born with history arranged beneath their feet. They earned it the hard way.

The Battle Itself

The surviving records fracture when they reach the field. Some describe a war zone already broken open before the first strike landed. Some speak of shattered stone, blackened ground, and a sky that felt heavy enough to collapse. A few claim the Old Six faced a host. Others insist the enemy was fewer in number but far worse in kind. Whatever the exact truth, every account agrees on the same core fact: the fight should have ended with six deaths and a silence afterward.

Instead, the battle became an act of refusal. The Old Six endured where endurance should have failed. They pressed forward long after reason said stop. Their names did not disappear into the dead. They cut themselves into memory so violently that the world never managed to forget them again.

The Moment They Became Legends

This battle marks the line between a party with stories and a company that became the standard for legend. Before it, they were remarkable. After it, they were the comparison every future hero would suffer under. Every impossible boast invites the same response: you are not the Old Six.

That is what the battle did. It transformed six adventurers into a measure. Later generations inherited not only their fame, but also the weight of everything they proved could be survived, opposed, or destroyed. In Sinners, that kind of proof is never harmless.

What History Lost

Time has blurred the edges. Too many witnesses died. Too many stories were passed through mouths that preferred awe to precision. That is why the archive cannot pretend to give you a perfect military record. It cannot count every strike or map every step across the field. What it can give you is the truth that remained when all lesser details eroded.

Six figures entered a catastrophe and emerged unforgettable. One of them, 922, would later become bound forever to the story of Pride. The others left scars of their own across history, whether in legacy, rumor, or unfinished consequence. The battle ended, but the shape it carved into the world never really did.

Why This Entry Endures

The Battle of the Old Six hangs in the gallery because it teaches an important lesson about this world. Not all legends are ancient because they were cleanly recorded. Some are ancient because the world keeps circling back to the place where they were born, trying to understand how six lives became something larger than time.
