Tales Character

Eustace

A player character remembered through Overgod's Revenge.

Known History

Eustace is a 30 year old man with beautiful blonde hair and pristine silver armor. He’s the most attractive man in the party (CANONICALLY) and is pretty much everything anyone wants to be.

He started his adult life at the ripe age of 18 as a machinist, inventing many gadgets like the original lasers and power armor. He spread good tech around each of the 7 major countries, opening a workshop in each of them and when humanity took his inventions and used them for ill, he sealed his workshops and began the life of a noble, heroic, and very handsome adventurer. He adventured for 10 years, got bored of that because he was too good at it, and then took a year off before meeting the party.

Legacy

A man known by many names, Eustace, Abram, and finally Patience, he would eventually become one of the many beings remembered as the Virtues.

Though Patience was slain by the party during the final battle, death was not the end of his influence. His soul endured long enough to rescue his fallen comrades, elevating them beyond mortality and transforming them into gods.

Through this act, an entirely new pantheon was born.

The Virtues now exist as an opposing divine force to the Sins, though they are neither as prominent nor as powerful. Their influence is scattered, carried primarily through isolated churches, cults, and zealots who continue to worship them across the plane.

Many of these groups remain dangerous.

Some see the Virtues as saviors who were wrongfully destroyed. Others believe they represent a purer form of divinity than the Sins. Whatever their beliefs, cults devoted to the Virtues continue to appear throughout the world, causing unrest, violence, and chaos wherever their influence takes root.

And so, despite dying in the battle meant to end his threat, Patience succeeded in ensuring that the Virtues would outlive him.
