The Continent of Sin

Rushinghan

City of Lust

The jewel of the continent: temples, art, relationships, rebellion, and a culture built around caring without apology.

At the Table: Arriving in Rushinghan

Your players enter a city that feels alive in every direction. Music spills from open doors, temples rise above crowded streets, artists work in public, couples argue and reconcile in the same plaza, friends gather around food and drink, and nearly every neighborhood feels decorated by the people who live there.

Rushinghan does not hide emotion well, and it does not particularly want to. Affection, desire, grief, celebration, romance, friendship, anger, art, and personal expression are all treated as things worth showing.

The Jewel of the Continent

Rushinghan is one of the great cultural centers of the Continent of Sin. Its reputation comes from art, nightlife, religion, relationships, public celebration, and the sheer amount of life packed into its streets.

A City of Temples

Major temples dedicated to many of the Sins stand within Rushinghan, making it one of the most important religious centers on the continent. Lust's temple remains central, but the city is not religiously isolated from the rest of The Pantheon.

The Passions

Lust's followers, known as the Passions, shape much of the city's culture. Their faith emphasizes love, connection, consent, affection, and the freedom to choose who and what matters to you.

The Rebellion

Rushinghan became the heart of resistance during the rebellion against Pride. Tess emerged as one of the city's most important figures, helping turn emotion, anger, fear, and loyalty into organized resistance.
