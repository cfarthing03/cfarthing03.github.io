The Continent of Sin

Rushinghan

City of Lust

The jewel of the continent: temples, art, relationships, rebellion, and a culture built around caring without apology.

The Jewel of the Continent

Rushinghan is one of the largest cultural centers on the Continent of Sin. Its streets are defined by art, music, relationships, nightlife, religious celebration, and a willingness to care about things loudly rather than pretending detachment is sophistication.

A City of Temples

Rushinghan is unusual because its skyline contains major temples dedicated to many of the Sins, not only Lust. These structures are among the most elaborate churches on the continent. Even among them, Lust's great temple remains the city's religious centerpiece.

The Passions

Followers of Lust are commonly known as the Passions. Their religion emphasizes love in every form: romantic, familial, communal, artistic, platonic, and personal. Choice and consent are central to their theology, especially because Lust's own history includes the creation of the Rixel Virus.

Love Beyond the City

Passions frequently travel outside Rushinghan, spreading aid, kindness, affection, and their understanding of connection. That missionary work tends to look less like conversion and more like becoming involved in the lives of the communities they visit.

The Rebellion Against Pride

Rushinghan became the heart of resistance during the rebellion against Pride. Tess emerged as one of the city's most important figures and helped transform its emotional intensity into organized action. Rushinghan did not rebel because it stopped caring about the gods. It rebelled because its people cared enough to fight.

Life in Rushinghan

The city can be indulgent, extravagant, chaotic, and exhausting. It can also be extraordinarily warm. Rushinghan's deepest cultural assumption is that attachment is not weakness. If something matters to you, you are expected to admit it.
