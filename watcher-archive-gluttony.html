The Fifth Voice

Gluttony awakened as a piglet in his representative coloration.

Pride's first surviving assessment does not ask what Gluttony thinks or understands. It asks what can be put into him and what comes back out.

Pride // G-05 utility assessmentSubject converts consumed matter and failed magical products into recoverable energy with unusually low loss. Potential solution to waste problem. Further tests required.

If stable, G-05 may permit repeated experimentation without equivalent resource expenditure.

Where Pride saw waste reclamation, Gluttony experienced eating things no one else wanted to touch.

Gluttony // deposition G-3

I was hungry when I woke up.

That sounds funny now.

He gave me a tray.

I thought it was food.

Some of it used to be alive.

I learned not to ask what was on the trays.

RECLAMATION G-05Inventory comparison

Cleanup

Failed experiments stopped being expensive once Gluttony could consume the remains and return usable energy.

Broken constructs. Spoiled reagents. Mutated tissue. Burned magical residue. Samples Pride no longer needed. Entire test organisms when an experiment produced a result he considered useless.

They went to Gluttony.

The Watchers found 612 disposal entries in one surviving ledger. Only 44 identify what was disposed of. The rest say reclaim through G-05.

REC-G05-112 // disposal batchstatus: partially legible

Contents: failed growth culture, contaminated crystal lattice, biological remains from ██████, unusable tissue from S-01 sampling.

Return efficiency: acceptable.

G-05 vomited after second intake. Reintroduced material after recovery.

Watcher note: The final line is written in a different ink, likely added after the session.
Gluttony

The worst part wasn't taste.

People always ask about taste.

The worst part was knowing “failed” meant Pride didn't care what it used to be.

The Room Where Nothing Was Allowed to Be Waste

The reclamation room was built for efficiency. Stone floor. Drain channel. Weighing table. Feed containers. Conduit assembly. Nothing decorative, nothing soft, nothing that acknowledged the person expected to sit in the center and consume whatever Pride pushed toward him.

Gluttony remembers the room by smell.

Gluttony // deposition G-4

Metal first.

Then burnt magic. Hard to describe that one. Like lightning if lightning could rot.

Then blood sometimes.

Then the soap Pride used after.

I still don't like that soap.

Failed biological experiments were the worst because they were rarely clean remains. Some still twitched. Some regenerated badly while waiting to be reclaimed. Some were recognizable enough that Gluttony asked whether they were dead and stopped asking after Pride began answering, “Functionally.”

Watcher investigators found claw marks beneath the edge of the central platform. Gluttony says they are his.

Gluttony

I tried not to eat once.

He waited until I was hungry enough.

Then put more in front of me.

People think hunger makes choices simple.

It just makes every choice louder.

The Battery

After Pride confirmed the energy conversion was repeatable, Gluttony became part of the correction system.

Whenever another Sin required prolonged restraint, regeneration, temporal manipulation, or forced magical output, Pride could feed failed material into Gluttony and pull the resulting energy directly into the apparatus. The process made torture cheaper.

Pride positioned Gluttony where he could be monitored. That also meant Gluttony could see the person receiving the energy.

Gluttony // deposition G-6

I knew everyone's sounds.

That's not a sentence I like.

Wrath got quiet when he was really hurt.

Lust talked until she couldn't.

Sloth—

Subject stopped. Interviewer offered to end session. Subject declined.

Sloth would say “no” before Pride asked the question.

Like he could get ahead of it.

Gluttony repeatedly asked Pride to move him where he could not see. Pride refused. One note explains why: visual exposure “appears to improve G-05 compliance.”

Guilt was another tool.

Energy extraction physiologyCorrection apparatus notes

Being Used Did Not Feel Neutral

Pride's logs describe Gluttony as a power source so often that later historians sometimes repeated the phrase as though it were metaphorical.

It was literal.

Conduits were attached to his body and the energy recovered from consumption was pulled out faster than he naturally released it. The process left him shaking, nauseated, and ravenously hungry. Pride solved the hunger by feeding him more reclamation material, turning the procedure into a closed loop: consume the remains of failed experiments, convert them, drain the conversion into a machine hurting someone else, repeat.

POWER-G05 / sustained drawG-05 output declined after 17 minutes. Increased feed volume restored flow. Subject requested pause due to abdominal pain and tremor. Pause denied while correction apparatus remained active.

Observation: visual contact with recipient increases distress but does not reduce output.

Gluttony remembers trying to look away from the table. Pride moved his chair.

Gluttony

I thought if I didn't watch, maybe I could pretend the energy went somewhere else.

He put me where I had to see.

He liked lessons.

“It Wasn't You”

Gluttony's testimony repeatedly describes himself as participating in what happened to the others. The others do not use the same language.

Gluttony

I powered it.

You can say I didn't choose it. I know.

The machine still ran because of me.

Personhood reconstructionRoutine logs

Counting Everyone

Small details are what make Gluttony's file feel least like a laboratory record and most like the beginning of a family.

He began keeping track of who had returned from experiments. If someone was missing, he did not settle. He saved anything remotely edible until he knew whether Lust wanted it. He sat near Sloth after correction sessions even when Sloth was too exhausted to speak. He learned when Wrath wanted company and when Wrath wanted space. Envy complained that Gluttony checked on him too often and then quietly started leaving notes where Gluttony would find them.

Pride recorded most of this as inefficient attachment behavior.

Gluttony

I started counting.

One, two, three, four.

Then me made five.

If there were only four, I stayed awake.

Didn't help anything.

Still stayed awake.

This is the strongest surviving origin for Gluttony's later obsession with family, companionship, food shared rather than merely consumed, and the difference between being full and being satisfied.

Waiting for Six

By Gluttony's awakening, Envy's escape preparation was already extensive. Five conscious Sins knew one relic remained.

Gluttony later said this was the first time the group felt like it was waiting for a person they had never met.

Gluttony

We didn't know Greed.

Still couldn't leave him.

That's family too, I think.

Saving a place before someone gets there.

Envy needed the facility ready. Wrath needed a usable temporal window. Lust needed Sloth mobile enough to survive the attempt. Gluttony needed to preserve enough energy that Pride could not simply starve the escape of power.

They waited.

Closing assessment

The Hunger He Chose

Pride defined Gluttony as a mouth attached to a recycling system.

Gluttony defined himself by what he refused to let be disposable.

Food became sacred because feeding someone is the opposite of consuming them as waste. Family became sacred because a person is not a failed experiment to throw away. Enjoyment became sacred because life should contain something beyond surviving the next use Pride found for your body.

Gluttony // final line of deposition G-9

I like when everyone eats together.

Hard to think you're disposable when somebody saved you a seat.
