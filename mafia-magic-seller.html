Person of Note

The Don

A feared black-market magic dealer whose merchandise is exceptional, whose curses are unavoidable, and whose patience should never be tested.

Archive Record

The Don is one of the most feared magic sellers in the black market. If an item is difficult to acquire, unusually powerful, or simply unavailable through respectable merchants, there is a good chance someone will eventually tell you that The Don can get something better. His merchandise has earned a reputation for being among the highest quality the black market can offer.

That quality comes with two prices. The first is gold. The second is whatever curse happens to be attached to the item you purchased.

The Price of Quality

Every item sold by The Don is cursed. This is not an occasional defect, an unlucky shipment, or something his buyers discover only after a particularly bad purchase. It is simply part of doing business with him. The curses are not generally dangerous in the traditional sense. They are rarely designed to kill their owners or render the item unusable. Instead, they range from mildly irritating to spectacularly inconvenient.

One buyer might discover that an item causes their body to produce pheromones that make them unusually attractive to squirrels. Another might find that every time they jump, a fish falls directly onto their head. Neither curse is particularly lethal. Both become significantly less amusing after living with them for any meaningful length of time.

That is the trade. The Don offers remarkable magic at black-market prices, but nothing leaves his possession completely clean. Anyone buying from him knows that the item will work. The uncertainty is what else it will make part of their life.

Reputation

The Don's reputation extends far beyond the quality of his inventory. Stories surrounding him describe a figure who does not tolerate disrespect, threats, theft, or people who decide that the rules of his business do not apply to them. Those who seriously upset him are said to disappear entirely or return so badly mangled that recognition becomes difficult.

Whether every story is true hardly matters. Enough of them are believed that most people entering his business already understand the expected behavior.

The people working for The Don are numerous and fiercely loyal. His men carry the same unmistakable image wherever they go, dressed in sharp tuxedos and conducting themselves like old-fashioned mafia enforcers. The formal clothes, controlled posture, and practiced professionalism make it clear when you are dealing with one of The Don's people long before anyone says his name. Whatever inspires that loyalty, it is strong enough that threatening The Don himself is only one of the problems a reckless customer would create. His organization extends well beyond the individual conducting the sale, and those around him treat his authority as absolute.

The Don Himself

All of this makes the first meeting somewhat difficult to reconcile with the stories.

The Don is a small black kitten.

Nothing about his size changes how the room treats him. His employees defer to him, his customers watch themselves around him, and conversations stop when he decides they should stop. It quickly becomes clear that the kitten is not a mascot, a familiar, or an animal belonging to someone more important nearby. He is The Don.

Pointing out that he is a cat is considered an exceptionally poor decision. Calling attention to his size, treating him as harmless, or otherwise suggesting that his appearance makes him less worthy of the reputation surrounding him is one of the faster ways to discover why that reputation exists.

How He Speaks

The Don does not conduct negotiations through ordinary speech. Instead, a collection of small boards bearing pictures is kept near him. When he wishes to communicate, he selects the appropriate board by bapping it with one of his tiny paws. A translator then tells the other party what The Don has said.

The process can look absurd to someone seeing it for the first time. That impression usually does not last. Everyone around him treats each bap with the same attention they would give a spoken command, and the translator delivers his meaning without hesitation.

Whatever else can be said about The Don, the method works. Deals are made, prices are established, warnings are understood, and very few people need the same warning twice.

At a Glance

Role: Black-market magic seller

Merchandise: High-quality cursed magic items

Appearance: Small black kitten

Communication: Picture boards and translator

Warning: Do not point out that he is a cat.
