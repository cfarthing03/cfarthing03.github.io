The Seven Sins

Envy

Longing, comparison, aspiration—and noticing what everyone else missed.

Envy is one of the most cunning of the Seven Sins. His divine form is not a single body, but a congregation of countless souls woven together into the faint outline of a man.

The God Who Watches

Envy appears as hundreds of thousands of souls intertwined into the faint green outline of a man. The form is fitting. Envy is a god of attention, comparison, longing, and the uncomfortable awareness that someone else possesses something you do not.

That does not make him a god of simple resentment. Modern interpretations of Envy focus on what comparison can produce. Seeing someone accomplish something extraordinary can inspire bitterness, but it can also produce the thought, I want to be able to do that too. Envy lives in that moment.

The Architect

Envy was among the quickest of the Sins to recognize what Pride was doing. His interference played a major role in the Sins' ascension and later helped orchestrate Pride's second demise. He is rarely remembered as the loudest person in a conflict. More often, Envy is the one who noticed the detail everyone else ignored and moved the right piece before anyone understood there was a game being played.

His most enduring divine innovation is the Sinner's Mark. Envy devised the means through which each of the Seven Sins could grant a mortal a direct portion of divine power. The individual gods still decide who receives their own Mark, but the system itself exists because Envy built it.

The Sinner's Marks

Only one Sinner's Mark from each Sin can exist at a time. A Wrath Mark and an Envy Mark can coexist, but there cannot be two living bearers of Wrath's Mark at once. There is also no requirement that all seven Marks be active. A god may go years or generations without choosing anyone.

The reason a person is selected is rarely obvious to outsiders. Bearers and the gods who chose them generally understand more than everyone else, while the public is left with theories that never quite explain every case.

Etro and the Radio Tower

Etro expresses Envy through entertainment. Music, celebrity, theater, spectacle, competition, and performance dominate the city, while the Radio Tower broadcasts entertainment throughout society through specialized pins. Anyone entering Etro is required to wear one without exception.

The city is built around watching and being watched, listening and being heard, seeing what others can do and deciding what that makes you want to become. The Green Circus carries the same idea across the continent: admiration and jealousy do not have to end in resentment. Sometimes they are simply the beginning of practice.
