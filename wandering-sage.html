Person of Note

The Wandering Sage

The elder who walks the continent one church at a time, carrying with him the authority to decide who rises within them.

The Keeper of Promotion

The Wandering Sage holds an authority that reaches across every church on the continent. When a member of a church is to rise in rank, the decision ultimately passes through him. He alone determines who is permitted to advance within their respective church, and when the time comes, he personally conducts the ceremony that makes that advancement official.

That authority has made him one of the most respected elders and leaders of the Church of the Old Six. His influence is not confined to one city, one congregation, or one faith. Every church on the continent falls within the circuit he maintains, and every promotion waits upon his arrival.

Seven Years Around the Continent

Despite the reach of his office, the Wandering Sage does not travel with a grand procession. He walks. City to city and church to church, he crosses the continent on foot, arriving when a congregation's turn has finally come. He performs the promotion ceremony, recognizes those he has chosen to advance, and then almost immediately begins the journey toward the next city.

A complete rotation takes seven years. By the time he returns to a church, an entire seven-year cycle has passed since its previous promotion ceremony. For those waiting to rise within the church, his arrival is therefore not a routine administrative visit. It is the only opportunity they will receive for years.

The Ceremony

When the Sage arrives, those eligible for advancement are brought before him and the promotions are carried out under his authority. Once the ceremony is complete, he does not linger to enjoy the celebration or the reverence attached to his office. His work in that city is finished. Another church waits somewhere farther down the road, even if its ceremony is still a year away.

This endless circuit has defined him as much as the power of his position. He is a leader of every church, yet belongs fully to none of them for long. His office is measured in roads walked, ceremonies completed, and the seven years it takes before the same doors open for him again.

The Envelopes

For all the respect surrounding the Wandering Sage, there is one story about him that no church has managed to explain. At seemingly unpredictable moments, a portal opens beside him. From it comes a small envelope. Inside is a picture.

No one appears to know what the pictures depict. The Sage has never offered an explanation, and those around him are given little time to understand what they have witnessed. Whatever is on the image is enough to break through the composure of one of the most respected religious leaders on the continent. Every time an envelope arrives, the reaction is the same. He screams in agony.

Then the portal is gone, the picture remains with him, and eventually the journey continues.

Who sends the envelopes, what the images show, and why they have such power over him remain unanswered. Considering the position he holds and the number of people whose futures depend upon his judgment, the most common conclusion is also the simplest: at some point, the Wandering Sage must have angered someone capable of making sure he never forgets it.
