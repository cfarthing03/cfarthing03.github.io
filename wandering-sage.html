Person of Note

The Wandering Sage

A traveling Passion priest who once served as a direct connection between Lust and the people.

Archive Record

The Wandering Sage is a priest under the Passion order. It is said that at one point in time, he was said to be a direct connection between Lust and the people. Now his role is simply to promote those within the church. He travels city by city on foot to hand out these promotions to those he deems worth. Once he leaves a city it usually takes him around 6 years before he returns.
