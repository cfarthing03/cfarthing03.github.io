The Continent of Sin

Palin

City of Warriors

Wrath's desert city, where strength is public, combat is ritual, and citizenship is something many people literally fight to earn.

The Colosseum

Palin is organized around its enormous Colosseum. The arena is simultaneously a civic institution, religious space, training ground, entertainment venue, and place where disputes can be resolved.

Citizenship Through Combat

A person seeking full citizenship may be required to prove themselves in the arena. These fights are dangerous, but not usually intended to be fatal. Arena staff intervene when necessary, and surviving the test is meant to demonstrate resolve rather than suicidal recklessness.

Wrath's Blessing

Those who fight in Wrath's name while within Palin can be sustained without ordinary food or water. In a desert environment, that blessing shapes daily life as much as religion does.

More Than Brutes

Outsiders often assume Palin is populated by simple-minded fighters. In reality, the city is disciplined, structured, and politically sophisticated. Strength is respected, but so are control, judgment, and the ability to use force for a reason.

Justice Through Strength

Wrath's influence gives combat a moral dimension. Palin's ideal warrior is not someone who attacks the weak. It is someone strong enough to confront injustice and disciplined enough to know when force is actually necessary.
