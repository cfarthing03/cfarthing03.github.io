The Continent of Sin

Palin

City of Warriors

Wrath's desert city, where combat is worship, civic ritual, justice, and sometimes the price of citizenship.

The City of Warriors

Palin rises from one of the harshest regions on the continent. Its people are famous for physical strength, but the city is not simply a gathering place for people who enjoy fighting. Discipline, justice, resilience, and the controlled use of force are central to its culture.

The Colosseum

The Colosseum is Palin's defining landmark. Political disagreements, personal conflicts, religious rites, public celebrations, training, and formal challenges can all pass through the arena. Fighting here is not automatically an attempt to kill. It is a language the city understands extremely well.

Citizenship in the Arena

Those seeking full citizenship may be required to prove themselves in combat. Arena staff monitor the fights closely and intervene when a contestant approaches the point of death. The purpose is to demonstrate resolve and capability, not to fill the arena with corpses.

Wrath's Blessing

Wrath's influence has reshaped survival itself. Those who fight in his name while within Palin can be sustained without ordinary food or water. In the middle of a desert, that blessing is more than theology. It is infrastructure.

More Than Strength

Visitors often arrive expecting a city of brutes and leave surprised by how organized Palin actually is. Its government, traditions, and systems are built around the idea that strength without judgment is worthless. A warrior who cannot control themselves is not impressive; they are dangerous.

Justice

Wrath's modern worship places enormous importance on directing anger toward something worthy of it. The ideal citizen of Palin is not the person quickest to throw a punch. It is the person strong enough to act when action is necessary and disciplined enough to stop when it is not.
