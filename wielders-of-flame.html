Wielders of Flame

The blue-flamed followers of the Great Flame whose search for Vale became the first stage of Sol's destruction.

Followers of the Great Flame

The Wielders of Flame served the Great Flame, one of the separated pieces of Greed. Their identity was tied so closely to that master that even their fire carried a distinctive color. Blue flame marked their presence, their banners, and eventually the memory of one of the most destructive afternoons in Sol's history.

The group was not searching for wealth or territory. Their defining assignment was a person: Vale. At the time, Vale was the human form of Greed and did not understand what he truly was. The Great Flame wanted him found and brought in.

The Search for Vale

That search turned the Wielders into a moving pressure across the places they entered. Vale could hide because he believed himself to be an ordinary man caught in extraordinary circumstances. The people hunting him did not share that misunderstanding. To them, finding him was not a favor and not a rescue. It was an order.

When the search stalled, patience ran out above them.

Sol Burns Blue

The Great Flame became convinced that Vale was hiding in Sol. Rather than continue searching house by house, he ordered the town razed. The Wielders obeyed.

Blue fire spread through Sol. The attack devastated the city and turned the search for one man into collective punishment against everyone who happened to live around him. The Great Flame did not need every resident to know where Vale was. He only needed the possibility that Vale was among them to make the city itself expendable.

Sol survived the first destruction badly wounded. It would not survive the afternoon intact. Later that same day, the Serpent struck and finished what the Wielders had begun.

What the Wielders Became in Memory

The Wielders of Flame are now inseparable from the destruction of Sol. Their blue fire became more memorable than whatever victories or duties came before it. They are an example of what happens when an organization is built around obedience to a being powerful enough to turn impatience into policy.

They were not the only force responsible for Sol's fall. They were the first half of it.
