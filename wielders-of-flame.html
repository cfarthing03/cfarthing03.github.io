Faction

Wielders of Flame

Followers of the Great Flame whose hunt for Vale helped ignite the destruction of Sol.

Known Record

The Wielders of Flame served the Great Flame, one of Greed's separated pieces. Their task was to locate Vale, Greed's reincarnated human form, and bring him to the Great Flame. When Vale failed to appear, the Great Flame ordered the organization to raze the town where Vale was believed to be hiding. Their attack devastated Sol, and later that same afternoon the Serpent finished the city off.
