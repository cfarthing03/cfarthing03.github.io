Tales Character

Sesku

A player character remembered through Echoes of the Velvet City.

Known History

Sesku Voidborne is a Yuan-ti explorer whose life became defined by the apparent deaths of his wife Candisa and their children Terra and Loodle. Their strange illness followed one of Sesku's discoveries, and his refusal to accept their loss drove him into a search for a cure and an explanation. His family history also ties him to the House of Jennestas and to forces interested in the Great Tree.

Legacy

Sesku's journey continues, and the consequences of his actions have not yet fully revealed themselves. His story is not over.
