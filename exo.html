Known History

I remember it like yesterday, I was five at the time. Me and my mom were watching my dad fight in the Colosseum. He was winning like he always does. He was winning. then one wrong misstep and suddenly he was just laying there…. In a pool of his own blood….. I didn't think about it at the time but it looked like his shadow pushed him, breaking his stance…. Anyways it all went downhill from there. It doesn't help that during that time weird things had been happening around me… like two people were fighting over me. Things would always break around me but always seem to repair themselves after a while. This got me in trouble all the time and my mom always blamed me for it even when I told them I didn't do it. Well, my mom never liked me in the first place. So when she started drinking this weird red liquid after dad died and sold me off to an orphanage to get more of it; I wasn't shocked.

That wasn't the end of my problems. Back then I went through a major mental breakdown after dad died my mom started being rougher with me and started saying things no kid should be told things like “mistake, you shouldn't have been born, it's’ your fault he's dead”.....” monster” That last one stuck with me. So throughout my stay at the orphanage I had to deal with that thought process “was I a monster, am I the reason dad’s dead, I shouldn't be alive”. it doesn't help that during that my magic started revealing itself; really doubling down on my emotions. Thankfully there was someone there who was able to get me out of my funk. It was one of the other orphans. Her name is Tess. I'm surprised she managed to get to me. I was really scared that if I touched anyone they would die…. But she managed to get me out of that mindset and convinced me that I wasn't a monster. After that, I stuck around her like a fly is to rotten bread. She's like an older sister to me. She taught me most of the things I know today. While she was helping me with my magics’ she asked me how I came about my magic so she brought me a book about the ‘history of magic for dummies’ which told me about how magic came to be about how you can be born with it or how you can learn it. The last one taught me about how some people pray to the gods to gain magical powers. Now I already knew about the gods and how people prayed to them (I mean why else do we like to fight so much if not for one of the gods) But I thought they were fake, you know, legends stories you tell your children to keep them in line, not something real enough to give someone magic if they prayed hard enough (I think it didn't tell specifically how they got them). In the end, all it told me was that I was too dumb to learn how to, not special enough to be born with it, and how I'm not praying hard enough to be given it. But that's the thing, the only logical thing is that the gods are why my powers. So after that learning experience, I had a lead but I was Ten and couldn’t do much so I just had to tough it out till I was old enough.

The orphanage wasn't bad, hell I could have made more friends but since I stuck around Tess like I was attached to her I didn't really make any friends. So other than reading every book they had and hanging around Tess I practiced, practiced my magic, Trained my body, learned how to throw a punch, and learned how to handle a knife. Tess helped me with those last three a lot, strangely. I asked her how she knew so much and she told me that her brother had been teaching her how to do those things then she told me to keep it a secret but I didn't focus on that I said ‘had’ and she froze for a sec and then gave me a sad look and said ‘yeah…. had’ I said ‘oh….’ I didn't ask for more than that after that. It was when I was Nineteen when I asked what his name was. I asked because I told her about my dad and how he died, she told me his name was ‘Therion’ and how he went missing after leaving to go adventuring but everyone thinks he died so they put her in the orphanage instead of waiting for proof. I gave her a hug after that even though she told me she didn't need one but I smiled because she still hugged me back in the end. After that, it was just business as usual. Then I was Twenty.

“You know it’s weird that I'm finally gonna leave this place,” I say “yeah well it's not like you can stay anymore it’s either you become caretaker or leave and I caretaker,” Tess says. We're in my room packing my bags. There wasn't much so we got it done quickly. As we start to finish packing I ask “Tess-...” I hesitate I feel like I already know her answer “yeah Exo what's up” she asks I hesitate a bit more before “I- yo- you know you could always come with, explore the world with me while I look for answers um- ah- we could look for your brother while we're out there you said he was missing that doesn't mean dead we could always look ask people sent u-'' Tess stops me with a hug “Exo you know if I did that you wouldn't focus on your task and you know I thank you for even thinking that far…. it’s just that I'm already a caretaker here I can't just leave. Legacy

Once the wielder of Wrath’s Sinner’s Mark, Exo would eventually become known throughout history by another name: Ashwing.

Ashwing is worshipped by the Church of Rebirth, a faith devoted not directly to Wrath himself, but to Wrath’s Chosen. Though Ashwing’s original name has long since been lost to history, ancient stories claim that each of the Sins once possessed a Champion, mortals gifted extraordinary abilities so they could fight for and protect their respective nations.

Wrath’s Champion is now remembered only as Ashwing.

Countless stories are attributed to him. Some claim he could control the Sun itself, pulling its fire from the heavens and wielding its power as a weapon. Others tell of battles in which entire armies were reduced to ash before him. However, his most famous legend, and the one that would eventually become the foundation of the Church of Rebirth, tells of Ashwing dying in battle, only to rise again from his own ashes in the form of a phoenix.

The Church still recognizes Wrath as their god, but their devotion is focused primarily upon Ashwing, remembered as the great Protector of Palin and the mortal who embodied Wrath’s strength without becoming consumed by it.

Followers of the Church believe that death is not necessarily an ending. Those who live with the same conviction, courage, and devotion as Ashwing may one day prove themselves worthy of following in his footsteps and rising again as a phoenix after death.
