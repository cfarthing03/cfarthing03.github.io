The Continent of Sin

Oro

City of Innovation

The former Dead Zone transformed into the continent's center of machinery, invention, Automatons, and modern Pride.

At the Table: Arriving in Oro

Your players do not walk into Oro. They arrive by machine. Airships drift toward the city from above while trains cut through the surrounding region below. The moment they enter, machinery becomes part of the scenery: moving platforms, workshops, lifts, mechanical devices, Automatons, and inventions that may not exist anywhere else yet.

Oro feels like a city that is unfinished on purpose. Something is always being built, tested, repaired, improved, replaced, or proudly demonstrated.

From the Dead Zone

Oro was built in territory once known as the Dead Zone. Transforming that place into one of the most advanced cities in the world is central to its identity.

That history also connects Oro to the oldest stories surrounding the Relics of Sin and the shattered forge where they were first created.

A City Without Roads In

Oro can only be reached by train or airship, both technologies invented within the city. Airship travel is the more common route, and the city's skies function almost like another city's major roads.

Machinists and Automatons

Machinists and Automatons make up much of Oro's population. Workshops, factories, repair houses, laboratories, and private inventors fill entire districts.

Automatons are ordinary members of city life here. Their presence is so common that visitors who expect them to be treated as curiosities often find Oro strange for the opposite reason: nobody is impressed simply because an Automaton walked into the room.

Modern Pride

Oro reflects the current God of Pride through creation, achievement, identity, craftsmanship, and the satisfaction of making something worth being proud of.
