Oro

City of Innovation

Once the Dead Zone, now one of the most technologically advanced places in the world.

From Dead Zone to Metropolis

Oro occupies land once known as the Dead Zone and rebuilt itself into a city defined by invention and machinery.

A City You Cannot Walk Into

The city can only be entered by train or airship, both technologies created there.

Machinists and Automatons

Most residents are either Machinists or Automatons, though the city is not closed to others.
