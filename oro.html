The Continent of Sin

Oro

City of Innovation

The former Dead Zone transformed into the continent's center of machinery, invention, Automatons, and modern Pride.

From the Dead Zone

Oro occupies territory once known as the Dead Zone. What had been a scar on the continent was rebuilt into a city devoted to invention, craftsmanship, and technological progress. The transformation itself is one of Oro's proudest achievements.

A City Without Roads In

Oro cannot be reached by simply walking through a gate. Travelers enter by train or airship, and both technologies were created within the city. Airship travel is by far the most common method, making Oro's skies as important as another city's roads.

Machinists

Machinists form one of Oro's dominant populations. Engineering, mechanical construction, experimentation, repair, transportation, and automaton design are woven into ordinary employment. New devices frequently appear in Oro years before they become available elsewhere.

Automatons

Automatons are not treated as unusual visitors in Oro. They make up a large portion of the city's population alongside Machinists, with other peoples filling out the remaining districts. This has made Oro one of the easiest places in the world for an Automaton to simply exist without immediately becoming a spectacle.

Technology Without Equal

Among predominantly human cities, Oro is the most technologically advanced place in the world. The major exception is the dwarven presence in Sol, whose engineering competes with and sometimes surpasses Oro's work.

Modern Pride

Oro fits the current God of Pride far better than it ever fit the old one. Creation, achievement, identity, improvement, and the satisfaction of building something extraordinary are celebrated openly. The city does not ask its people to be flawless. It asks them to make something worth being proud of.
