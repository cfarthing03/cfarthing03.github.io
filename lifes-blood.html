Forbidden Substance

Life's Blood

A drug engineered by Dr. Rayabond to make dependency itself into a weapon against humanity.

Known Effects

A drug that once taken heightens you to the maximum capabilities of a human. However, it also alters your DNA to require you to take it at least once every month or it will kill you. This was created by Dr. Rayabond to help speed up the extermination of humanity.

If not taken the drug provides the following side effects: Scratchy/dry skin Bloodshot eyes Headaches Drowsiness (after the heightened abilities) and Bloodlust
