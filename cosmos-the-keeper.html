Person of Note

Cosmos the Keeper

A daughter and fragment of Greed who preserves an unbiased record of everything that has happened.

The Keeper of All Knowledge

Cosmos is the daughter of Greed and one of his five separated pieces. Her purpose is preservation. Anything that has happened throughout history is stored, maintained, and recorded by her.

The Library

Greed created a small pocket dimension known simply as the Library for Cosmos. Within it, history is written as it actually occurred, without national propaganda, religious revision, or the distortions that accumulate over generations.

Cosmos cannot see the future. She can, however, make frighteningly accurate predictions by comparing the habits and patterns she has watched repeat throughout history.

The Contract of Noninterference

Her contract with Greed limits how freely she can interfere in mortal affairs. Except in circumstances severe enough to threaten the world itself, Cosmos observes and records rather than acts.
