The Ruins

Before Oro became the City of Innovation, this ground belonged to Evorburn and to the ruins Pride left behind.

Evorburn

The Ruins are what remained of the kingdom of Evorburn after its destruction. Pride's surviving robots once ruled over the broken city and its shattered structures.

What Stands There Now

The site did not remain a ruin forever. Oro, the City of Innovation, now stands where Evorburn once did. Modern streets, workshops, rails, and inventions occupy ground layered over the remains of the old kingdom.

For that reason, Oro and the Ruins are not two distant places on the map. They are two versions of the same place, separated by history.
