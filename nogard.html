Person of Note

Nogard

An old merchant whose little shop becomes stranger the longer anyone remains inside it.

Archive Record

A simple weapons seller who loves to trick unsuspecting customers into poor deals. A great Hagler and an even better salesman. To challenge him is certainly a battle of wits and muscle one will certainly lose. Not that he'd let you know that.

An old man that knows more than he lets on. Runs a simple magic item store known as Nogards Treasure Trove and is ready and willing to do business with anyone that has coin. However the longer you remain in his shop the more and more bizarre things get.
