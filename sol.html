Sol

The Sleeping City

A nocturnal city rebuilding itself from devastation while old Sloth traditions and new dwarven technology collide.

Life After Sunset

Sol's social rhythm is reversed. The city sleeps through much of the day and becomes active after dark.

The Serpent

A colossal serpent erupted from the heart of the city and claimed to act in Pride's name, devastating Sol before being petrified.

Rebuilding

Modern Sol is slowly being rebuilt, with dwarven-controlled districts introducing advanced technology.
