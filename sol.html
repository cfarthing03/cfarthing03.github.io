The Continent of Sin

Sol

The Sleeping City

A nocturnal city rebuilding itself from devastation while old Sloth traditions and new dwarven technology collide.

Life After Sunset

Sol's social rhythm is reversed. The city sleeps through much of the day and becomes active after dark. Night is when shops open, communities gather, festivals begin, and the city feels most alive.

A Culture of Rest

Sloth's influence made rest part of civic identity rather than a private necessity. Sol historically treated sleep, recovery, affection, and nighttime community as natural parts of a healthy life.

The Serpent

That life was shattered when a colossal serpent erupted from the heart of the city and claimed to act in Pride's name. Its attack devastated Sol until the Pantheon defeated it and transformed the creature into stone.

The Dwarven Presence

In the years following the destruction, dwarves moved into parts of Sol and established some of the most advanced technology outside Oro. Their presence has changed the city's architecture, infrastructure, and political balance.

Rebuilding

Sol is no longer simply a ruin. The city is rebuilding slowly, district by district. Old traditions are returning alongside entirely new technology, making modern Sol one of the strangest mixtures of memory and reinvention on the continent.
