The Continent of Sin

Sol

The Sleeping City

A nocturnal city recovering from annihilation while Sloth's old culture and new dwarven technology grow together.

The Sleeping City

Sol's daily rhythm is the reverse of most civilizations. Daylight is quiet. Doors close, streets empty, and much of the population sleeps. As the sun disappears, the city wakes.

Nightlife as Daily Life

Festivals, markets, gatherings, meals, parties, and ordinary work happen beneath the moon. Visitors often describe Sol as a city that never sleeps, which is technically the opposite of the truth. Sol sleeps plenty. It simply does so while everyone else is awake.

A Culture of Rest

Sloth's influence made recovery and rest part of civic philosophy. Sleep is not treated as laziness by default. Sol historically valued affection, comfort, companionship, and the belief that a person should not need to destroy themselves simply to prove they are useful.

The Serpent of Pride

Sol was nearly erased when a colossal serpent erupted from the city and declared itself an instrument of Pride. The creature devastated entire districts before the Pantheon stopped it and transformed it into stone. Its remains became a monument to the city's destruction.

The Dwarven Presence

After the catastrophe, dwarves established control over portions of the ruined city. Their technology is among the most advanced in the world and now shares space with Sol's older architecture and traditions. Only Oro rivals or exceeds what can be found in these districts.

Rebuilding Sol

Sol is no longer a dead city. Reconstruction continues slowly, neighborhood by neighborhood. Old residents are returning, new communities are forming, and the city is becoming something neither its pre-destruction inhabitants nor the dwarves could have predicted.
