People & Legends

Not every name survives in stone. Some remain in witness accounts, tavern stories, old warrants, religious records, or a scroll someone had the good sense not to burn.

People
Nogard

An old merchant whose little shop becomes stranger the longer anyone remains inside it.

Je-Sin-perdy Host

A traveling host who offers dangerous trivia, strange games, and generous prizes.

Shazam

A legendary horse remembered for joining the war against Pride.

The Champion of Palin

A would-be champion betrayed by The Pantheon and eventually driven into Pride's service.

The Don

A black-market dealer in cursed magic items whose security policy leaves very little room for mistakes.

The Wandering Sage

A traveling Passion priest who once served as a direct connection between Lust and the people.

Cosmos the Keeper

A daughter and fragment of Greed who preserves an unbiased record of everything that has happened.

Dandi's Brother

The kidnapped brother of Dandi whose fate became entangled with the Relic of Sloth.

Dr. Rayabond

The angel responsible for Life's Blood and one of the most devastating attempts to exterminate humanity.

Robert

A Hus drug dealer who turned against Life's Blood and eventually bore Gluttony's Sinners Mark.

The Great Flame

A blue-flame fragment of Greed whose impatience helped turn one afternoon in Sol into a catastrophe.

The Inventor

The creator of Pride's robot army who eventually turned against his former master.

The Samurai

A rogue yokai-wielding adventurer who tried to kill Pride and was remade into one of Pride's soldiers.

The Serpent

The antagonistic fragment that represented Greed's body, deceived Vale, and finished the destruction of Sol.

The Spirit of Wind

A free-roaming fragment of Greed who surrendered her beloved freedom when Greed returned.

The Voice of Thunder

The fragment that gave Greed a voice powerful enough to bind gods and terrify mortals.

The Owl Wizard

A powerful forgotten wizard who once wielded the Relic of Wrath and had a fondness for Crown of Madness.
