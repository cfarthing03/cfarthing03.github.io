Tales Character

Trace

A player character remembered through Overgod's Revenge.

Known History

Life: Trace lived in a small western-style village. He lived there since he was 16 when he took up an apprenticeship from the gunsmith who lived there. He died at the age of 28 in a stick-up at his shop. Before he moved to this town, he was raised by his mother, who was barely around due to taking on multiple jobs to get Trace educated. He had not many friends, but they were good ones. They went off to high end colleges out of town when they all graduated.

Death: Right as he was shot in his shop, he experienced a flash and opened his eyes, and found himself in the Shadow Plane. He wandered there for the better half of a year before he met a hooded figure, who offered to bring him back to life in exchange for his service as a Grim Reaper. He accepted and was brought back as a Fetchling who was called the Undertaker by commoners across the country.

Rebirth: After he was brought back, he awoke in the middle of the Unknown. After he travels back to his shop in Palin, he makes it to town just as he died in his previous life, and he watches his killer run outside, and stares at him in horror. Trace instinctively pulls his pistols out of their holsters, and points them at the man, who shoots Trace in the chest, but it phases through him. Trace is about to pull the triggers when he immediately feels an immense pain in the back of his head and almost passes out. He looks up and sees a number over the killer's head that says 3 days.

Job/Expertise: He worked as a Gunsmith who supplied weapons and cannons to the capital and military. He trained as a Gunsmith under Gorot who was his mentor from an early age. Now as a Grim Reaper, he can sense when one is going to die soon, though he does not always know how soon it will be.

Religion: He worships Pride, despite living in Palin

NPC: Gorot & Alice

Gorot: Trace’s mentor and father figure who apprenticed him in the art of gunsmithing. He is in his late 60’s but is still very fit. He is a gunsmith and a bladsmith who has forged many weapons and blades. Alice: Alice is currently 7 years old, and is Gorots adopted daughter. She has picked up a few things about smithing from her father, but does not have the strength for it, so she just helps him out by sitting at the front desk and taking customers, fetching parts, stuff like that.

Legacy

Trace was once the Embodiment of Wrath, the chosen Grim Reaper of the god, entrusted with carrying out Wrath's will over death.

Eventually, however, Trace came to reject the idea that death should belong entirely to the gods.

He separated himself from Wrath and began following his own understanding of what death should represent.

Today, Trace wanders the wastes alone.

He does not kill indiscriminately, nor does he simply collect those whose lives have ended. Instead, Trace decides for himself when a soul's time has truly come.

Sometimes this means taking a life.

Other times it means protecting one.

He has intervened in deaths ordained by gods, prevented souls from being claimed prematurely, and quietly corrected countless supernatural disasters left behind by divine interference.

By every traditional measure, Trace possesses enough authority and power that he could declare himself a god.

He refuses.

To Trace, godhood would only place him inside the same system he has chosen to stand apart from.

So he remains something else.

A wanderer.

A reaper without a master.

A quiet force moving through the forgotten places of the world, cleaning up the mistakes of gods who may never admit they made them.

Most mortals have no idea Trace exists.

Among the gods, however, his name is known.

And despite his willingness to defy them, many have come to regard him not as an enemy, but as a force of good, someone willing to protect the natural boundary between life and death when even the gods themselves fail to respect it.
