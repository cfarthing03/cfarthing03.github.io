Tales Character

Lex the Watcher

A player character remembered through Clockwork Resurgence.

Known History

Lex was born in the city of Etro. He grew discontent with the people he was surrounded by, chasing dreams and an easy life while the world around them suffered, and vowed to not grow up to be like the vacuous citizens of Etro. When he came of age, rather than making it big in the theater as his parents expected, he left his life behind and moved to Palin, the City of Warriors, to gain the strength to protect those in need. He worshiped the god of Wrath and trained in both the use of weapons and blessings bestowed to him from his patron. With these tools at hand, he moved to the city he believed he could do the most good in Hus, the city of Gluttony. Not wanting for choice in crime to stop and plots to foil, Lex had many run-ins with the Dragons Tail, Thieves Guild, and Lifes Blood in the following years. The most disturbing of them was Lifes Blood, which released a drug that will kill you if you stop taking it. He’s devoted his life to bringing the Lifes Blood down. In his quest to defend the weak, the guards of Hus have given him the nickname Lex The Watcher, which he carries with pride. If a crime happens without his interference, he takes it personally, and will not hesitate to make it right, as violent as he needs to be

Legacy

Lex would eventually become remembered simply as The Watcher.

Following his adventures, he founded an organization known as The Watchers, a vigilante group dedicated to protecting the people of Hus when traditional authorities either could not, or would not.

Unlike armies or formal law enforcement, the Watchers operated independently, traveling wherever they believed people required protection. Their methods were not always considered legal, but their reputation among ordinary citizens grew rapidly.

Lex himself would eventually earn another title:

“The One Who Commands Gods.”

The name originated from his knowledge and use of True Names, allowing him to exert power over beings that most mortals would never dare challenge. Over time, the stories surrounding Lex transformed him from a vigilante into something closer to a legendary figure, a mortal who could stand before gods and command them by name.

His influence would survive even his own death.

Lex was later revived as an Angel, returning to the world with power far beyond what he possessed in life. Rather than submitting to the beings who restored him, however, Lex turned that strength against them.

During the events of Overgod’s Revenge, the legendary Watcher returned once again, using his angelic might to fight alongside the heroes against the forces threatening the world.

The organization he created continues to bear his name, ensuring that even when Lex himself is absent, someone is always watching.
