The Isle

An empty shore. An uneasy crossing.

A place left alone

The Isle is abandoned. Few travel here, and fewer can explain why they turn away. As you approach its shores, unease settles in before you have found anything to fear.

Rumor holds that the island is sacred to Envy, and that the god protects it dearly. Most are content to leave that rumor untested.
