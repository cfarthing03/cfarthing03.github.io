Xan Mera

A player character remembered through Tales of the Old Six.

Known History

As a child, Xan was abandoned as a kid, being the offspring of a werefox and a human, the mother was disgusted. He was found and raised by wolves until around the age of 7. He was found by a group of hunters who took pity on him. They fed him and taught him how to talk and speak. He took frequent trips to the Underdark with traders from his "clan", and so he saw and heard of many horrors. On one of these trips he got lost down one of the paths. But when he returned, he could not recall what happened down that path. When he reached the age of 17, he was wandering the forest gathering plants and food to eat, when he stumbled across an old hut, where he met an old Druid named Drur. He taught Xan the ways of magic and combat. This was all under the nose of his "clan" he was living with, for they were against the ways of magic. When he turned 20, Drur gave him a special visor that he had been keeping for years, called the Pollinator's visor. Xan treasures this greatly, and barely ever takes it off. A few days after this, his clan was fed up with Drur's nonsense, for he was very very old. So they went to confront the old man and killed him and burnt down his house and the surrounding wildlife. This enraged Xan, who then went on to kill all of the males in the camp, including the hunters who saved him. He then wandered the wilderness, not knowing where to go or what to do. He would wear baggy cloaks and masks to keep people from seeing who he really was. He would go to random inns and taverns taking jobs or bounties. On one of his adventures, he found one of his favorite items, the Rift Sheathe. Xan prefers not to kill women and children under the age of 16 and refuses to harm any wildlife of any kind. If it is tamed, however, that is a different story. He will do what he must to survive and has learned to rarely trust anyone. He does everything for his own benefit and well-being.

Legacy

Xan Mera became a legendary hero whose name survived long after the adventures that made them famous. They are remembered as one of the Old Six.
