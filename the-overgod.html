The Overgod

Some truths about the gods are dangerous. Others are dangerous because they reveal that the gods are not the highest thing in the room.

The Office Above Divinity

An Overgod is not simply another god within The Pantheon. The role exists above the ordinary divine order and is bound to the continued stability of the realm itself. Even describing it as a throne is misleading. A throne can be abandoned. This cannot.

A Vacancy That Could Not Remain Empty

When an Overgod was slain, the world could not simply continue without a replacement. Odin was forced to choose who would take the vacant place. He chose his wife.

She became the new Overgod and has endured the burden of holding the realm together ever since. Odin spends much of his remaining life beside her, waiting for the possibility that another being may someday be able to take her place.

What This Record Allows You to See

The archive can describe the office. It can preserve names, consequences, and fragments of succession. It cannot make the scale of the thing comfortable. The further the record is followed, the less the world resembles a collection of cities and gods, and the more it resembles something suspended inside a structure too large to perceive all at once.

You are not seeing the whole truth. You are seeing the amount of it that has been permitted to remain visible.
