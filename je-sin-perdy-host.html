Person of Note

Je-Sin-perdy Host

The renowned traveling host of Je-Sin-Perdy, a mysterious game that can appear almost anywhere there are people willing to play for dangerous questions and generous rewards.

The Traveling Host

The Je-Sin-perdy Host travels the land looking for people willing to play. He is not tied to a single city, hall, or permanent stage. The game goes where he goes.

That mobility is part of what makes an encounter with him unusual. Je-Sin-Perdy is not something most people simply visit. It finds contestants.

Je-Sin-Perdy

The game itself is mysterious. What is consistently known is that it revolves around trivia and strange challenges presented by its host.

The host’s presence turns the encounter into something more than casual entertainment. The promise of reward gives people a reason to keep playing even when the game becomes dangerous.

The Prize

Those who play and win are given generous rewards. That fact alone has allowed the game’s reputation to travel ahead of its host.

For anyone tempted by what might be won, the bargain is simple enough to understand: play the game, survive whatever it asks of you, and earn the prize.
