The Seven Virtues

Kindness

Mercy, unity, preservation, and the terrifying promise that no one will ever have to be alone again.

Kindness is the red crystal god: an elegant white-and-black figure whose head has become a jagged eruption of crystal surrounding a screaming skull.

The Promise of Kindness

Kindness begins with an argument almost everyone can understand: people suffer because they are divided. Nations fight. Families starve while others hoard. Fear becomes hatred because one person can never fully understand another.

The Crystals

According to the scriptures of the Messengers of the Chosen, Kindness created the red crystals that appeared thousands of years ago. Their purpose is not merely to heal or empower. They spread his influence and bring separate people closer to one shared existence.

One Collective Body

The cult's final promise is unity. If every person can be joined into one collective body, war, famine, loneliness, and misunderstanding can be ended at the source. Individuality is treated not as sacred, but as the wound from which cruelty grows.

Mercy Without Separation

That philosophy is why Kindness is so disturbing. His followers can sincerely speak about love, preservation, family, and peace while participating in the destruction of individual autonomy. In their theology, becoming part of the whole is not death. It is rescue.
