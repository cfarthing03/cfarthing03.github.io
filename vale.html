Tales Character

Vale

A player character remembered through Clockwork Resurgence.

Known History

The surviving archive contains only fragments of this life.

Legacy

Vale was never truly separate from Greed.

He was a lost piece of the Sin, broken away from the greater whole and allowed to exist as something resembling an individual.

For a time, Vale traveled the world as his own person, possessing only a fraction of what Greed truly was. His existence became one of the rare examples of a fragment of a divine being developing its own experiences, relationships, and identity apart from its source.

Eventually, however, Vale returned to Hilnem.

There, the lost fragment reunited with Greed, restoring what had once been separated and making the Sin whole again.

Yet Vale did not simply disappear.

Some believe the experiences and memories he gathered while separated became part of Greed himself, subtly changing the Sin after their reunion.

With his purpose fulfilled, Vale's presence settled permanently within the city of Hilnem, the home of Greed and the place where the fragmented pieces of his existence were finally made whole.

Whether Vale should be considered dead, absorbed, or simply returned home remains a matter of interpretation.
