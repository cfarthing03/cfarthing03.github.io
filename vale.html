Vale

A player character remembered through Clockwork Resurgence.

Known History

The surviving archive contains only fragments of this life.

Legacy

Vale was Greed's mortal reincarnation and, for a time, existed without understanding what he truly was. His path eventually returned him to Hilnem and to Greed.
