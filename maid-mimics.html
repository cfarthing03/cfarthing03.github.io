Faction

The Maid Mimics

A government-backed mimic maid cafe that became a revenge-bound faction after The Pantheon slaughtered its members.

Known Record

An entire group of mimics got together to form a maid cafe. Rather than living in dungeons and scaring people, they wanted to provide joy and laughter to the world. Not only was this government-approved project funded by the Church of Gluttony in order to provide better relations between the common monster and humanoids. It was heavily praised for its good food and friendly staff. They were generally loved by everyone! That was until "The Pantheon", a group of adventurers each possessing a sinners mark, murdered them all in cold blood. This left the mimics that survived to swear an oath of revenge. Tracking down the party to their very last breath.
