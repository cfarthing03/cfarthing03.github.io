Faction

The Maid Mimics

A government-backed mimic maid cafe that became a revenge-bound faction after The Pantheon destroyed the life its members had built.

A Cafe Made of Mimics

The Maid Mimic Cafe was not a cafe staffed by mimics wearing maid outfits. The cafe itself was mimics. The chairs were mimics. The tables were mimics. The plates and silverware were mimics. The walls around the guests were mimics. Nearly everything a customer touched, sat on, ate from, or walked past was alive. The food was the exception.

The servers were stranger still. Several mimics would combine their bodies into a roughly human shape, dress that shape as a maid, and serve customers together as a single person. The illusion was never meant to convince anyone that the server was truly human. The point was that everyone knew exactly what they were surrounded by and came anyway.

The project was government approved and received support from the Church of Gluttony as an attempt to improve relations between humanoids and creatures usually treated as monsters. Rather than asking mimics to hide what they were, the cafe made their nature the center of the experience.

And It Worked

People loved it. The food was good, the living furniture became part of the attraction, and the maid servers were memorable enough to make the cafe a destination rather than a curiosity people visited once. For a time, the experiment did exactly what it was supposed to do. Humanoids willingly walked into a building where almost every object around them could have teeth and treated the place as somewhere to relax.

That success is what made what followed so much worse.

The Massacre

The Pantheon arrived and slaughtered the Maid Mimics. Whatever justification may have surrounded the encounter, the event entered the surviving record as the destruction of a group that had deliberately abandoned the life everyone expected of them and built something peaceful instead.

Not every mimic died. The survivors did not return to serving tables.

The Oath of Revenge

The surviving Maid Mimics turned themselves into a faction defined by revenge. They tracked the members of The Pantheon and carried the memory of the cafe with them. The maid imagery remained, but what had once been part of a strange hospitality experiment became a reminder of what had been taken from them.

The Maid Knight became the most enduring figure attached to that second chapter. Long after the original cafe was gone and the immediate revenge campaign had passed into history, the Maid Knight continued to roam the continent. Reports still place the armored maid figure on the road, a living remainder of an organization that was supposed to have ended generations ago.

What Remains

The cafe is gone, but the experiment it represented is still remembered. It proved that mimics and humanoids could occupy the same space without disguise, cages, or extermination. For a while, people ate meals from living plates, rested on living chairs, and chatted with servers made from several mimics working together. That ordinary acceptance makes the massacre harder to separate from everything that followed.

The Maid Knight is the clearest surviving remnant. She still roams the continent, long after the original cafe and the faction born from its destruction should have passed entirely into history. Whatever purpose keeps her moving now, the sight of her is proof that the story never ended as cleanly as the records imply.
