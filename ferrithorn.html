Tales Character

Ferrithorn

A player character remembered through Tales of the Old Six.

Known History

Was a legend of his tribe a warrior who could take on the form of a bear and trample anyone who was in his path, his people celebrated him, and his opponents feared him. His tribe continued to shower him with love and approval. So in return, Ferrithorn continued to harness the power of the bear to protect the people of his home however one day after using it too much he lost control and killed his family. This caused the tribe to turn their back on him. Casting him outwards into a strange and unknown world and so he began traveling around doing whatever jobs people would hire him more.

Legacy

Ferrithorn became a legendary figure whose deeds continued to be remembered throughout history. They are counted among the Old Six.
