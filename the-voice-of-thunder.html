Person of Note

The Voice of Thunder

One of Greed’s five separated pieces, and the terrible voice that allowed Greed to bind gods, address higher beings, and force mortals into submission through sheer fear.

One of Greed’s Five Pieces

The Voice of Thunder is one of the five pieces of Greed. It is exactly what the name promises: a booming voice powerful enough to shake mountains.

The fragment represents more than volume. It gave Greed the ability to make his words matter against beings who could not be approached like ordinary mortals.

A Voice for Gods and Higher Beings

The Voice of Thunder allowed Greed to create contracts with gods and other high beings.

For a power whose society and influence became so deeply tied to magical contracts, that voice was essential. Greed was not limited to bargains among mortals. His words could reach the divine.

Fear as Authority

The same voice could be turned downward. When Greed needed mortals to submit, the Voice of Thunder could frighten them into doing so.

A sound capable of shaking mountains did not need to prove itself twice. Sometimes the threat was contained in the voice alone.
