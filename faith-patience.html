Religion of Patience

The Faith of Patience

The organized worship of Patience, built around endurance, waiting, perseverance, forgiveness.

Core Teaching

Followers train themselves to endure fear, grief, anger, delay, and uncertainty without allowing those emotions to choose their actions for them.

Ordinary Worship

The faith does not require every follower to become clergy. Common devotion is expressed through silence, vigils, delayed judgment, acts of forgiveness. The act matters most when it reflects deliberate commitment rather than public performance.

Temples and Clergy

Temples of Patience use the same visual language seen in the god's iconography: halos, eyes, white wings, and radiant gold. Clergy are expected to embody the virtue publicly, which makes failures of doctrine especially politically and spiritually significant.

Miracles and Divine Favor

Miracles attributed to Patience tend to be interpreted through the virtue itself. A believer does not merely ask for power; they ask for the ability to become more completely aligned with Patience's ideal.

The Dangerous Edge

Its harshest sects can transform endurance into obedience, teaching people to remain in situations that should be challenged.
