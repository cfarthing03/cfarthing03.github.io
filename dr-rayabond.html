Person of Note

Dr. Rayabond

An angel who sided with Pride, created Life’s Blood, and came terrifyingly close to turning a plan for human extinction into reality.

An Angel Who Chose Pride

Dr. Rayabond was an angel saddened by the forgetfulness of the people. In pursuit of regaining godhood, he joined forces with Pride.

The alliance did not remain theoretical. Rayabond became responsible for one of the most devastating weapons ever turned against humanity.

Life’s Blood

Rayabond created the substance known as Life’s Blood. It was designed as a drug that could spread through as many people as possible before becoming lethal.

The intention was extermination. The drug was not meant merely to poison isolated victims or terrorize a single city. It was meant to infect humanity widely enough that, when activated, the result would be annihilation.

Activation

The Pantheon intervened, forcing Rayabond’s plan forward sooner than intended. Even rushed, the activation succeeded on a catastrophic scale.

Humanity was not completely eradicated, but the population was reduced by approximately 65 percent. Failing to achieve total extinction did not make the result anything less than a world-changing disaster.

The Advantage Pride Needed

The devastation caused by Life’s Blood left Pride with the advantage he needed to continue his own plan to exterminate all life. Rayabond’s work weakened the world before the final confrontation had even arrived.

Rayabond was eventually slain during the final battle against Pride. By then, however, the consequence of what he had created had already spread far beyond him.
