Person of Note

Dr. Rayabond

The angel responsible for Life's Blood and one of the most devastating attempts to exterminate humanity.

Archive Record

An angel, saddened by the people's forgetfulness, joined forces with Pride in order to regain godhood. As a result, the angel created a substance known as Life's Blood, a drug with the intention of infecting as many individuals as possible before turning lethal, ultimately leading to the annihilation of humanity. Despite the Pantheon's intervention hastening his plans, the angel succeeded in activating the drug, albeit only achieving a 65% reduction in the population rather than complete eradication. Nevertheless, the widespread impact of the drug provided Pride with the advantage he needed to proceed with his scheme of exterminating all life. Ultimately, the angel was slain during the final battle against Pride.
