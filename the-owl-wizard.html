Person of Note

The Owl Wizard

A wizard of extraordinary power whose true name was lost to time, leaving behind only a title, a connection to the Relic of Wrath, and a strangely persistent fondness for Crown of Madness.

A Name Lost to Time

The Owl Wizard was a figure of great power. Their true name has been lost to time, and the title is what survived.

That loss makes the record feel incomplete, but it also reveals what history considered worth keeping. The name vanished. The power did not.

Wielder of the Relic of Wrath

The Owl Wizard was once the wielder of the Relic of Wrath.

That fact alone places the wizard beside one of the most dangerous relics in the archive. Whatever else has disappeared from the record, their connection to Wrath endured.

Crown of Madness

The surviving accounts also preserve a much stranger detail: the Owl Wizard liked casting Crown of Madness.

It is a small piece of personality beside a much larger reputation, but perhaps that is why it survived. History forgot the wizard’s name and remembered the spell they apparently could not resist using.
