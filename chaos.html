Tales Character

Chaos

A player character remembered through Echoes of the Velvet City.

Known History

Chaos, once Kairos Frostthorne and later known for a time as Gold, survived shipwreck, exploitation, illness, death, resurrection, and transformation into an Automaton. A former sailor and pirate, she made a life in Hus through performance, theft, and increasingly dangerous work. Fiercely independent and drawn to risk, she carries multiple identities, old debts, and a long history with the Artificer.

Legacy

Chaos remains an active piece of the world's unfolding history.

Where their path will lead, and what legacy they will eventually leave behind, has yet to be determined.

Her story is not over.
