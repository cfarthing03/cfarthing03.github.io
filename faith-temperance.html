Religion of Temperance

The Faith of Temperance

The organized worship of Temperance, built around moderation, restraint, balance, control.

Core Teaching

Followers build deliberate limits into ordinary life so appetite, ambition, grief, pleasure, and anger never become unquestioned masters.

Ordinary Worship

The faith does not require every follower to become clergy. Common devotion is expressed through measured meals, schedules, abstention rites, pauses. The act matters most when it reflects deliberate commitment rather than public performance.

Temples and Clergy

Temples of Temperance use the same visual language seen in the god's iconography: eclipses, circles, orbits, measured lines. Clergy are expected to embody the virtue publicly, which makes failures of doctrine especially politically and spiritually significant.

Miracles and Divine Favor

Miracles attributed to Temperance tend to be interpreted through the virtue itself. A believer does not merely ask for power; they ask for the ability to become more completely aligned with Temperance's ideal.

The Dangerous Edge

Its harshest sects can impose one authority's definition of moderation on everyone else and call emotional suppression virtue.
