Field journal / mineralogical anomalies / ten confirmed varieties

The Specimen Journal

The handwriting is quick, crowded, and increasingly excited.

ENTRY 01 / ARRIVAL

This is not a mineral survey anymore.

I came here expecting a geological curiosity. Ten colors later, I am no longer convinced “mineral” is even the correct word.

The crystals grow here and, as far as I can determine, nowhere else naturally. Yet every examination produces the same impossible conclusion: whatever rules formed them are not the rules of this world.

I should be frightened by that. I am, a little. Mostly I cannot stop thinking about what they might let us do.

ENTRY 03 / BLOOD RED

It grows when fed blood.

The red variety reacted to a cut on my thumb before I had even finished recording its mass. One drop touched the specimen and vanished into it. The crystal lengthened almost immediately.

Potential applications are obvious: self-growing structures, emergency barriers, tools that can be expanded when needed rather than transported at full size. I do not yet know whether animal blood and human blood produce different results.

I have transferred several samples to a colleague with better facilities for living-tissue work. They were extremely eager to continue the tests.

NOTE: establish a safe feeding limit before considering medical use.

ENTRY 07 / PINK

“Correction” may be the only word for it.

A fractured ceramic tile was placed against the specimen. The crack disappeared. Not sealed. Not filled. Gone. The repaired section matched the original glaze, including a flaw that should have been impossible for the crystal to know about.

A second test restored a snapped brass key to its prior shape. A third removed a dent from a watch casing without opening the watch.

If it behaves similarly with living tissue, this could change medicine completely. Bones restored rather than set. Scars reversed. Damaged organs returned to what they were before injury.

Question: what does the crystal consider “correct” when several prior states are possible?

ENTRY 11 / YELLOW

It separates.

The yellow specimen produces boundaries where there should be none. Mixed powders divide cleanly. Mud separates into water and dry sediment. Most astonishingly, a sealed chamber test produced a visible discontinuity in the air itself.

I keep thinking about purification. Clean water from contamination. Smoke pulled from breathable air. Toxins separated from food. Even mining could be transformed if useful ore could simply be persuaded to stop being part of the surrounding stone.

Do not place a hand across an active boundary again. Numbness persisted for forty minutes.

ENTRY 16 / BLACK

I saw the other side of the room without looking across it.

The black crystal does not cut matter. It cuts distance.

For less than a second, an opening formed above the table and connected to a point near the far wall. I passed a pencil through. It emerged three paces away before the opening collapsed.

Transportation. Rescue. Supply lines. Crossing oceans without ships. Reaching places sealed by disaster. The possibilities are so enormous that I have rewritten this page three times and still sound ridiculous.

CAUTION: the second aperture did not open to any location I recognize. Stop unsupervised testing.

ENTRY 21 / ORANGE

Heat and propagation.

Orange spreads its crystal structure through material in direct contact with it. The converted material becomes extremely hot but does not always ignite.

Controlled correctly, this could provide heat without conventional fuel, sterilize equipment, melt snow, smelt metal, or create temporary furnaces almost anywhere.

The problem is stopping it. A test rod continued converting after removal from the primary specimen and had to be isolated in sand.

Find a reliable termination method before increasing sample size.

ENTRY 24 / ICE BLUE

The orange crystal has an opposite.

Ice Blue also propagates through contacted matter, but removes heat rather than producing it. Water freezes almost instantly along the advancing edge.

Food preservation alone could save entire settlements from seasonal shortages. Medical transport becomes easier. Fires could be stopped before they spread. A paired Orange and Ice Blue system might even regulate temperature without ordinary fuel.

Wear insulated gloves. The sample cabinet hinge shattered after becoming brittle.

ENTRY 31 / PURPLE

Weight is apparently optional.

The purple specimen can reduce, redirect, and in some cases seemingly negate gravity within a small area.

I lifted a stone block larger than myself with two fingers. More accurately, I pushed it and waited while it continued upward.

Construction. Cargo. Mobility aids. Moving injured people without worsening spinal damage. Entire buildings could be assembled without cranes if the effect can be stabilized.

Tie everything down before activation. Including the ink bottle.

ENTRY 39 / WHITE / RESTRICTED

I think it changes time.

I do not mean perception. I do not mean aging alone. A pendulum placed beside the white specimen completed six swings while the reference pendulum completed twenty-three. On a second trial, the relationship reversed.

A wilted flower briefly returned to the condition it had been in that morning. The effect lasted nine seconds. When it ended, every petal fell at once.

The benevolent uses are almost too tempting to write down. More time for surgery. Preserving someone at the edge of death until help arrives. Reversing a few seconds of catastrophic injury.

But if a person can alter time for one thing, they can alter it for another. An army that does not experience the same hour as its enemy. A ruler who decides a prisoner's sentence should contain more years than the world outside.

No further testing alone. No removal from the Isle. Record every fragment. Count them twice.

ENTRY 44 / MAROON / RESTRICTED

This one frightens me.

Maroon permits direct control over another living creature.

The first successful test was performed on a beetle. I thought of left, and it turned left. I thought stop, and it stopped. When I released the effect it behaved normally.

I repeated the test on a mouse because I needed to know whether complexity mattered. It did not.

There are medical possibilities. Preventing a seizure. Interrupting involuntary movement. Guiding a panicked animal away from danger. I want those possibilities to be what this discovery means.

But I know what someone else will see the instant they read this page.

This sample should not leave my possession. I am not even certain this page should exist.

ENTRY 52 / GREEN

It heals.

No metaphor this time. Green crystal accelerates recovery and can suppress illnesses that ordinary medicine cannot touch.

A cut closed beneath the specimen in minutes. Diseased tissue in a preserved sample lost every visible sign of corruption while contact was maintained.

The effect is not permanent for severe disease. Remove the crystal and the condition begins returning. That limitation matters, but it does not erase what this could mean. A person who has weeks could have years. A disease that cannot be cured could still become something survivable.

I have spent days studying objects that bend distance, gravity, matter, and time. This is the first one that made me cry.

Find a way to extend the effect without permanent contact. There has to be one.

ENTRY 61 / PERSONAL

What are we supposed to do with miracles?

Ten colors. Ten impossible tools.

I keep imagining hospitals warmed by Orange, preserved by Ice Blue, supplied through Black apertures, with Green at every bed and Pink waiting for injuries that once meant a lifetime of pain.

Then I think about White. I think about Maroon. I think about the red samples I handed away because I was too excited to ask exactly what “living-tissue work” meant.

Discovery is not goodness. But neither is ignorance.

If these things can help people, someone has to learn how. I only hope the people who learn first are the people who still care who gets hurt along the way.
