The Specimen Journal

The handwriting becomes smaller as the pages continue.

A contradiction

These crystals originate on the Isle alone, yet they do not belong to this world. I keep returning to that distinction. A place of origin should explain a specimen. Here it only deepens the question.

Ten colors. Ten different violations of what matter ought to do. I had expected a mineral survey. I have found something far more interesting.

Blood Red

It feeds on blood, grows, and can produce crystal monstrosities.

Pink

It rewrites or corrects things.

Yellow

It separates matter, including air.

Black

It tears through space-time, opening portals or similar breaches.

Orange

It spreads crystal growth through material it touches and produces intense heat.

Ice Blue

It spreads through contacted material and produces intense cold.

Purple

It manipulates or negates gravity.

White

It manipulates time. Specimens are exceptionally rare.

Maroon

It permits control over another creature.

Green

It heals and can halt severe disease. Continued contact is required to maintain its effect.

An unfinished note

If this island is where they originate, what does origin mean for something that does not belong here?
