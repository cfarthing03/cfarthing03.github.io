Older Archive Note

A warrior chosen by Greed, this mark granted the user the following abilities:

1 The body of a reptile, Focus on fortitude and durability 2 The Breath of flame, focused on attack and damage 3 The knowledge of the cosmos, focused on reality-bending and containment 4 The Gift of flight, focused on transportation and manipulation, 5 The voice of Thunder, The intimidation and presence of a dragon 6 Contract Bind
