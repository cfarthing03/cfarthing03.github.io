The Continent of Sin

Etro

City of Dreams

Entertainment, illusion, music, and a criminal history hiding beneath a spotless surface.

At the Table: Arriving in Etro

The first thing your players notice is the music. It is already playing before the city fully comes into view, carried from the Radio Tower and caught by the pins worn throughout Etro. The streets are clean enough to feel staged, performers spill out from theaters and plazas, and every few blocks something seems to be happening: a singer in the street, dancers gathering a crowd, a public rehearsal, a promotion, a celebration, or someone trying very hard to become famous.

Etro feels like a city that knows it is being watched. People dress to be remembered. Businesses compete for attention. Posters, announcements, live performances, and public personalities are everywhere.

The City of Dreams

Etro sits apart from the rest of the Continent of Sin, both geographically and culturally. Its distance from the mainland helped it develop a strong identity centered on performance, entertainment, ambition, comparison, and spectacle.

It is the city most closely associated with Envy, not because everyone is bitter, but because Etro constantly places examples of success in front of people and quietly asks what they plan to do about it.

The Radio Tower

The Radio Tower is impossible to separate from daily life in Etro. Twelve songs are broadcast continuously through the city, and residents historically use specialized pins that carry the signal. Anyone entering Etro is expected to wear one.

To locals, the music is ordinary background noise. To visitors, it can feel like stepping into a city that has its own permanent soundtrack.

Life on the Streets

Etro's public spaces are built to be seen. Plazas are shaped around gathering crowds. Restaurants and shops use live entertainment to pull people inside. Performers practice in public because being noticed is part of the process.

Behind the Performance

Etro's polished image hides a much rougher history. The city has long supported a criminal underworld that learned how to thrive beneath clean streets and crowded venues. The historic Life's Blood trade found a foothold here.

The city can be genuinely beautiful and genuinely dangerous at the same time. Its greatest skill has always been presentation.
