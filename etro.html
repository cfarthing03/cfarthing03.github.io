The Continent of Sin

Etro

City of Dreams

A city of performance, spectacle, and carefully maintained fantasy—bright enough that its shadows are easy to miss.

A City Built on Performance

Etro is geographically separated from most of the Continent of Sin, but culturally it has never been distant. Once associated directly with Envy, the city became one of the continent's great centers of entertainment, performance, and public spectacle.

The Ratio Tower

At the heart of Etro stands the Ratio Tower, an enormous radio structure that broadcasts the same twelve songs throughout the city. Residents are expected to carry their city pins, allowing the music to remain a constant part of daily life.

The Underworld

Beneath the spectacle is a serious criminal history. Etro was once deeply involved in the trade of Life's Blood, and its entertainment industry created opportunities for exploitation as easily as fame.
