Overgod's Revenge · Unassuming Catastrophic Power

Mike

Mike was, by most ordinary measures, just a normal guy the party happened to pick up. The problem with that description is that Mike could control black holes.

A Normal Man

Mike did not enter the story with the bearing of a legendary hero or the authority of a god. He was simply someone the party met, brought along, and eventually trusted.

His ability made that normality difficult to maintain. Mike could manipulate black holes, shaping forces that should have been far beyond anything a mortal could safely control.

Constant Experimentation

Mike's greatest strength was not simply the raw power of his ability. It was how quickly he learned to use it in ways no one had expected.

Black holes became weapons, edges, barriers, and tools. He could shape them into swords and other forms rather than treating them as simple points of destruction. When his abilities later expanded to include white holes, his options became even stranger.

He learned to use white holes as platforms, walking across forces that pushed matter away, and constantly developed new techniques in service of the party.

Black and White

The combination of black holes and white holes gave Mike access to opposing forces: collapse and expulsion, attraction and rejection, destruction and movement.

His powers were flexible enough that their limits were often determined less by what they could do and more by what Mike thought to try next.

Power Without Ceremony

That contrast became part of what made Mike memorable. He could wield forces associated with the structure of reality itself while remaining, at his core, Mike.

He was not a god. He was not a cosmic office. He was a person helping his friends with an ability that had become terrifyingly large.

The Rupture Between Worlds

Eventually, Mike's power became part of something larger than any single battle. He served as a rupture between multiverses, a point through which separate realities threatened to remain connected.

To close that rupture, Mike collapsed in on himself.

The act sealed the multiverse away from itself and cost him his life. For now, the separation holds.

His final contribution was not another clever weapon or impossible platform. It was using the same forces he had spent so long learning to control to close a wound in reality that no one else could.
