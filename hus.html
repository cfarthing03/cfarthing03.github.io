The Continent of Sin

Hus

The One Stop Dinner

The largest concentration of people on the continent, held together by food, appetite, trade, and more crime than anyone likes to admit.

Food as a Way of Life

In Hus, food is not simply a necessity. It is culture, status, religion, work, celebration, and political power. The city's dense population supports enormous markets, kitchens, restaurants, and supply networks unlike anywhere else.

The Church and the Table

The Church of Gluttony has historically taken an unusually direct interest in the city's food supply. Ingredients, quality, access, and distribution are religious concerns because Gluttony's domain is woven directly into what the city consumes.

A History of Shortage

Hus has survived periods when food distribution broke down badly enough to threaten the entire city. Leadership failures inside the Church once drove shortages and prices upward until outside intervention helped stabilize the situation.

Crime Capital

The same density that makes Hus a culinary powerhouse also makes it the center of organized crime and drug activity. Life's Blood has circulated through the city for years, and criminal organizations thrive in neighborhoods where law enforcement, commerce, and desperation overlap.

Present-Day Hus

Modern Hus remains loud, crowded, delicious, dangerous, and difficult to ignore. The Church is rebuilding its position, while the city's underworld continues to treat every new opportunity as another market.
