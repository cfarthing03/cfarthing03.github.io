Sentient Construct Lineage

Automatons

Sentient machines born in the aftermath of Clockwork Resurgence, each carrying a crystal heart and a stubborn desire to prove that being manufactured does not make a life less real.

Born After Clockwork

Automatons emerged after the events of Clockwork Resurgence. They are not simple machines following elaborate instructions. They are sentient beings capable of thought, loyalty, fear, ambition, and choice.

Many revere Pride as the god who gave their kind life, even when the circumstances surrounding that creation are more complicated than the stories told among Automaton communities.

A People Without Birth

Automatons are rare because they cannot reproduce on their own. A new Automaton must be constructed by a highly skilled craftsperson and brought to life through work blessed by Pride.

Every new Automaton is deliberate. Someone must decide to build a body, obtain the necessary materials, prepare a core, and create another member of the lineage.

Proving Themselves

Many Automatons work hard to establish that they are people rather than property, weapons, or curiosities. Their small population means most people have never met one, and every Automaton knows that a single encounter may become someone's entire understanding of their kind.

Legacy

Their existence is one of Clockwork Resurgence's most permanent consequences. Armies and machines from that era eventually became history. Automatons remained, carrying the question of what happens when something built to serve learns that it can choose.

The Crystal Heart

At the center of every Automaton is a crystal power source mounted within the chest. Nine of the ten known crystal types can serve as a core. Blood Red is the single known exception.

When the core is inserted, channels traced throughout the Automaton's body ignite with the color of the crystal. The core does more than provide energy: it grants the Automaton an ability related to that crystal's nature. Replacing the crystal changes both the color of those channels and the ability available to the Automaton.

Without a crystal, an Automaton powers down. Their body becomes still and unresponsive in a catatonic-like state until a functioning core is returned to the chest. They do not die immediately, but they cannot move, speak, or meaningfully interact with the world while unpowered.

Change the crystal above to watch the core network reconfigure. Detailed Automaton manifestations of each crystal are still being catalogued.
