The Legend

A legendary warrior that harnessed the power of belief.

To a far-off hill in Sol laid a man. He was old and yet his aura poured off of his like hard liquor. Those who were unlucky enough to come across him knew to tread lightly. Legend says that a single glance into his eyes could permanently paralyze you. A flick could permanently disable you. This was a man above all others. One that could kill even gods. One day on the outskirts of Palin the man met with a small child. This child asked him a simple yet terrifying question. "Why are you so strong?". The man smiled as he turned to the young boy. An eerie grin spread from cheek to cheek. His eyes filled with rage. The man looked the boy in the eye and stated "Why to kill you... all of you who rest in this town..." he tapped the boy on the heart continuing his monologue. "...In 2 days your city will be nothing but ash and blood". The boy with a look of fear ran off to his town on the other side of the hill screaming for help.

2 days had passed before the man rose up once again. The same disgusting smile ran across his face. As he turned around his eyes glanced down towards the bottom of the hill where 70 warriors stood. Men, women, and even some powerful children had all gathered in front of the man. His eyes lit up with excitement as he asked. "What's with the party?" An older warrior stepped forward scars covering his whole body. His eyes locked with the man as he said "We have come to put an end to your terror, if you leave now there shan't be any bloodshed" The man on the hill laughed "so you've come to die then?" the man on the hill spoke with a darkened tone. The moon rose behind him in a bloodied form. Bright as the blood that was spilled. Towards the bottom of the hill stood the boy the man spoke to before. He crouched down to speak with him "This world is unfair, this world is cruel. This is something you would have learned if you weren't 71" before the boy could speak a claw had already run its course through his stomach. The boy spitting up blood muttered one final word before joining the rest of the corpses "y-your a devil" as he slumped over in the mans arms. He looked up towards the blood filled moon and spoke "A devil huh, I think I like that..."
