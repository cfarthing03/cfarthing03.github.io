Tales Character

Grimthar

A player character remembered through Echoes of the Velvet City.

Known History

Grimthar the Grave was born in a village outside Palin and grew up beneath the shadow of a father who equated Wrath with fear and punishment. His mother, Inga the Grave, taught him healing and herbalism, while Grimthar himself became fascinated with Wrath as a god of justice, bravery, and triumph. After Inga's death, Grimthar eventually became the village healer, trained heavily with a shield, and later left for Rushinghan carrying her title, "the Grave," rather than his father's "the Wrathful."

Legacy

Grimthar has since taken on the mantle of Disciple of Wrath, forging a much closer connection with the Sin than most mortals could ever hope to achieve.

What that title will ultimately mean for him, and for Wrath himself, has yet to be determined.

His story is not over.
