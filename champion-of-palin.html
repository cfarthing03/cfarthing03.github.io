Person of Note

The Champion of Palin

A man once poised to become Palin’s next champion, only to have that future ripped away by betrayal and replaced with a single purpose: vengeance against The Pantheon.

A Champion in Waiting

Earfed was once positioned to become the next champion of Palin. Before that future could become reality, a mechanical dragon attacked. Earfed fought back, and with the help of The Pantheon the creature was driven away and the city was saved.

For a moment, his path and theirs ran together. What should have been the beginning of a heroic legacy instead became the beginning of the grievance that would define what followed.

The Search for Farnear

Earfed joined The Pantheon when they set out to recover Farnear, whose soul had been captured in a jar by a witch after his death. The goal was simple in the way terrible goals often are: get their friend back, whatever it took.

The price became clear when The Pantheon traded Earfed’s soul for Farnear’s. Whatever trust had existed between them ended in that exchange. Earfed had helped save a city beside them, only to become the thing they were willing to surrender.

A Servant of Pride

The witch was revealed to be a servant of Pride. She brought Earfed to him, and the betrayal that had already taken everything from Earfed gave Pride exactly what he needed.

Earfed swore loyalty to Pride on one condition: that service would give him the chance to kill The Pantheon. The man who had once been expected to stand as Palin’s champion instead became an enemy shaped by the people he had once fought beside.
