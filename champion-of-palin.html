Person of Note

The Champion of Palin

A would-be champion betrayed by The Pantheon and eventually driven into Pride's service.

Archive Record

A man who was at one point going to be the next champion. However, was soon attacked by a mechanical dragon and with the help of The Pantheon drove off the creature and saved the city. He joined them on a quest to help get their friend Farnear back from a witch who captured his soul in a jar when he died. The Pantheon traded his soul for their friends, of which Earfed was not happy about. The old witch turned out to be a servant of Pride and brought Earfed to him. Earfed then swore loyalty to Pride if it meant killing The Pantheon.
