Tales Character

922

A player character remembered through Tales of the Old Six.

Known History

He was made 54 years ago by a lone intelligent goblin. This goblin wanted every race to be eliminated besides his own, and he was going to use warforged to do it. That is where he learned to kill and fight. Eventually, the goblin declared war on every race and was completely out of his league. 922 was ordered to watch over a hill and alert of advancing troops on that position. The war ended before anyone would walk on that hill. But 922 waited, waited for an enemy that would never come. 922 is unsure of whatever happened to the goblin Over the 48 years he sat there waiting his body and armor were covered and reclaimed by Mother Nature. One day someone did March on that position. A gang of humans with a 10-year-old tiefling, came marching up the hill. They wanted to hang the tiefling in a place where her body would never be found. 922 slaughtered them, except for the tiefling girl. He had never seen or killed a tiefling before, his goblin master had never shown him one. The girl thankful for being saved took him to his hideout where she cleaned most of the moss and vines off of him and gave him an animated shield, the last thing her parents had given her. She introduced herself as Zerra, and she saw something inside 922 Because for the next 5 years she would teach him emotions and how to live. Everything he would never have learned was given to him. Eventually, they moved to a town where they lived peacefully until one day while they were out Zerra was taken by slavers while 922 was distracted. All he saw was Zerra being rushed off in a cage away from the town. 922 decided he would stop at nothing to get her back. For two years he looked taking jobs for connections, info, or money. He will do anything to get Zerra back even if he has to buy her from the people who took her from him.

Legacy

922 would later become the Host of Pride. Their deeds became legend, and they are remembered as one of the Old Six, though their connection to Pride sets them apart.
