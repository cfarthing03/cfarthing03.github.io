The Second Voice

Lust awakened while Sloth still possessed a stable body.

Like the Sins who followed her, she recognized the already-awakened relic immediately as something like herself. Pride did not need to explain Sloth. Lust saw him and knew.

Her first independent form was small: a pink lovebird, bright enough to look absurd inside Pride's laboratory. Sloth's later recollections consistently linger on the color. Watcher analysts suspect that is because Lust was the first living thing in his memory that did not belong to Pride.

Lust

He stared at me like I'd walked in from another world.

I didn't understand why until later. He'd been waiting for someone without knowing he was waiting.

The friendship formed before Pride recognized it as dangerous. That friendship gave Sloth enough certainty to refuse Pride's intended use for him.

Lust then watched what Pride did to teach the first awakened relic the cost of refusal.

What Sloth Could Not Describe

Sloth's own account of the seventy-five years is made of sensations: tired, cold, hurt, again. Lust remembered the shape of it.

She describes Sloth restrained while Pride separated parts of him that still moved after separation. She remembers the wet impact of pieces placed into metal trays. She remembers Pride learning that Sloth could recover and becoming less careful because of it. She remembers the point at which recovery stopped meaning restoration and began meaning that the pieces merely found one another again.

Lust

People say he was torn apart because that is easier to say than what happened.

He was torn apart, and then Pride waited to see what still moved.

He learned what could be removed without making Sloth stop answering. He learned how long the screaming lasted. He learned how long it took for the pieces to crawl back.

Then he did it again with better notes.

Pride periodically allowed Lust to speak with Sloth. The timing was not kindness. Surviving logs show that these meetings frequently followed failed attempts to force cooperation. Pride appears to have believed affection could be used as leverage in both directions.

Pride // behavioral correction noteContact with L-02 improves S-01 responsiveness but does not improve obedience. Consider controlled access as reward rather than unrestricted interaction.

Lust learned the intended lesson anyway. Pride could be patient. Pride could be pleasant. Pride could also spend seventy-five years destroying someone who said no.

The Assignment

The Rixel Virus was commissioned after Sloth's rebellion.

Pride's reasoning is unusually explicit. Useful people were unpredictable because they could refuse instructions. Punishment was expensive, slow, and did not guarantee obedience. Pride wanted a contingency: a way to take a person who would not listen and make their body comply anyway. It would also serve as a threat. Cooperation could remain technically voluntary as long as everyone understood what happened after refusal.

He gave the project to Lust.

Pride // control-contingency directiveRequirement: persistent obedience independent of subject preference.
Requirement: host remains operational despite injury for maximum useful duration.
Requirement: mechanism propagates without repeated administrator intervention.

L-02 is to develop implementation.

Lust did not misunderstand what she was making. Pride's instructions were specific. She designed the mechanism knowing it would seize control of unwilling people. What she did not know was who Pride intended to use it on, how widely he intended to distribute it, or whether there was any limit he would recognize once it worked.

Lust

I knew what it did.

I need that written correctly. I knew.

I also knew what the room next to mine sounded like when Sloth said no.

Watcher copies of her later statements return repeatedly to the same point: fear did not make the work less hers. She never asks the reader to pretend she was unaware. She asks them to understand why she believed refusal would put her on Sloth's table.

Rixel Virus // Progression

The original virus remains the virus encountered today. Watcher comparison of later outbreaks against the oldest surviving descriptions has found no meaningful redesign. It is violent, volatile, and remarkably stable across time.

1 // TransmissionInfection begins when a person is stabbed by, pierced by, or otherwise comes into direct contact with one of the ribbon-like growths extruding from an infected host.
2 // EstablishmentThe organism spreads through the host and prioritizes keeping the body alive. Damage that should end useful function can be compensated for if doing so gives the infection more time to grow and propagate.
3 // EmergencePink ribbon-like structures begin appearing through existing openings and breaks in the skin, then create additional exits as the infection advances.
4 // ControlThe ribbons function like additional tendons. They pull directly against the body, overriding voluntary movement and forcing limbs into positions chosen by the controlling infection.
5 // ResistanceThe host may remain conscious and attempt to fight the movement. The ribbons do not yield. Bones, joints, and muscle fail first.
6 // PropagationExtruded ribbons become new vectors. Contact with another body creates the next opportunity for infection while the original host is kept alive as long as practical.

Accounts of active control are consistent in one detail: the victim's body can be made to move with enough force to injure itself. A resisting arm may be pulled until the shoulder dislocates. A planted leg may remain fixed while the rest of the body is forced forward until bone gives way. The virus treats those injuries as maintenance problems, not reasons to stop.

Watcher annotationThe infection's life-preserving behavior should not be mistaken for healing. It preserves the body as a vehicle. Survival is useful because living tissue can move, grow ribbons, reach new hosts, and remain controllable longer.

What She Chose Afterward

Lust eventually gained a humanoid form, but her oldest testimony still describes herself as the little pink bird who sat near Sloth because neither of them wanted to be alone.

The Watchers consider it difficult to separate her later domains from that history. Pride taught Lust how to create a connection that removed choice entirely. She became a goddess who treats choice as the condition that makes connection worth having.

Family. Love. Friendship. Desire. Community. Every one of them can be imitated by control from the outside. Lust's worship insists that the imitation is not the thing itself.

Lust

If you cannot leave, it isn't a bond.

It's a leash.
